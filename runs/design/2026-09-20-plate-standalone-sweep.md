## FAIL 02 — The Mood Mirror
Carrying element now: the two stacked lines. Should be: the vertical bar labelled THE GOVERNING PART, which is the only new thing in panel two.
- The bottom line is **broken** where the top is continuous. At ten seconds that is "disconnect from her, go cold" — the caption has to deny it in words ("Not to go cold"). The drawing argues against its own caption. **Close the bottom line: make it continuous, and put the vertical bar *through* it, not in a gap.**
- Relabel THE GOVERNING PART to **SOMETHING OF YOURS**. Untranslated faculty-language at ten seconds is a stop.
- Relabel THE SAME WEATHER to **HER WEATHER, DOUBLED** so panel one reads as loss, not symmetry.
- Cut the second footer line ("The day is still hers") — with the line closed, the first footer carries it.
Cost: label and geometry edits, same concept. One pass.

## FAIL 05 — The Remaining Nails
Cold read was the slowest of the ten, and it is the only plate whose cold sentence missed the Conversation sentence's real content ("that is how a man becomes small without deciding to").
- **The concept cannot stand alone; send it back to the concept stage.** Two causes, and they compound. First, it is Ch04's fence post redrawn, so a reader days later reads it as a repeat of the anger chapter, not as frank speech. Second, the floating sideways nail above the board reads as a nail about to go *in*, not one pulled out, and it sits nowhere near the dot it left.
- If a redraw is refused, minimum edits: move the floating nail directly above its dot; label it **pulled out**; label the four uprights **still in**; cut "Parrhesia" or gloss it inline as *saying it plainly, once you are calm*.
Cost: highest of the three. New concept, full cycle.

## FAIL 06 — The Tally You Don't Read Aloud
Carrying element now: the solid black bar. It carries the wrong man's work.
- **Polarity is inverted.** The dark, solid, visible bar is captioned "her hands moving, yours still," so black reads as *her* contribution and the ghost as *his*. The plate flips into "I do the invisible work and get no credit" — the self-pity position the subtitle exists to deny. **Swap the fills: make THE WEEK solid black and TONIGHT the dashed ghost**, and relabel TONIGHT's caption to **what she can see right now**.
- Collides with Ch07. Two consecutive plates titled THE TALLY YOU DON'T READ ALOUD and THE PRIVATE TALLY, both bar-and-ledger, both about counting. A reader one chapter a week will not know which he is in. **Retitle 06 to THE WEEK SHE DIDN'T SEE**, leaving "tally" to Ch07. (Publisher's note: the title is the distillation's Mechanism line, inbox #065; a retitle is a distillation change, which is the author's.)
Cost: fill swap, two labels, one title. Cheapest of the three.
