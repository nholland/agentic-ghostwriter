# Why This Is The Most Important New Source In The Round-2 Pass

Chapter 12's whole argument is that romance is the first thing cut and the cut
is invisible. Round 1 had no evidence for it and said so; it borrowed Ch11's
Randall & Bodenmann stress study by analogy and was told not to. This is the
finding that sits directly under the claim, in the right literature, at
meta-analytic scale: the two maintenance behaviours most about warmth and
reassurance are the two that run in the opposite direction from relationship
duration.

With Huston et al. (2001) beside it, the chapter can say the thing it has been
asserting — that the warm effort declines, and that the decline is what
matters — without borrowing another chapter's engine.
