# Why This File Exists

The round-1 brief ruled the five love languages out on the strength of one
review and one small study, and told the Ghostwriter not to name it. The
author has since ruled the opposite way, with a calibration. A researcher who
reports only the evidence that supported the earlier ruling is doing
advocacy. This is the evidence on the other side, and it is real.

It does not rescue Chapman. It changes "the matching hypothesis fails" — which
the chapter cannot print — into "the matching evidence is mixed and nobody has
tested whether changing your behaviour helps," which it can.
