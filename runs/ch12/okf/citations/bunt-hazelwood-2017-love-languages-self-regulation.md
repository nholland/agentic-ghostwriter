# Bunt & Hazelwood (2017) — Love Languages and Self-Regulation

Sixty-seven heterosexual couples assessed on love language preference,
self-regulation, and relationship satisfaction. Whether the two partners'
languages lined up made little difference to satisfaction. Each partner's
self-regulation did.

# Status

**Verifiable, not verified.** Abstract-level evidence only.

# Why It Matters for Ch12

It is the single study that most directly tests Chapman's central claim, and
it does not support it. Paired with the Impett review, the chapter is standing
on a review plus an independent empirical test rather than on one source.
