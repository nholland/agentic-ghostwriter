# Perceived Partner Responsiveness

The idea: what predicts how loved a person feels is not which channel the care
arrived through, but whether they came away believing their partner
understands what matters to them, values it, and acted on it. Responsiveness
is about accuracy of attention, not format of delivery.

Reported associations across the literature: greater intimacy and closeness,
higher satisfaction and commitment, more trust, greater relationship
stability, and knock-on effects outside the relationship (self-esteem,
personal goal pursuit).
