# Why It Fits

Chapter 12's reader is not in crisis. 04-archetype.md is explicit about that:
Enlightened Eric is curious and growing, not in trouble. The failure mode this
chapter describes is therefore not a fight, it is a flatness — and this is the
study that says flatness at year seven is visible at year sixteen. It gives
the chapter its stake without reaching for divorce statistics, which
06-sources.md and the bundle's own infidelity note both warn against.
