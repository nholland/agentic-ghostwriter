# Chapter 13 cold concept pick

## Initial readings — recorded before reading the distillation

- **A:** In ten seconds on a phone, a married man would take away that both partners keep changing and should trade what matters, ask follow-up questions, and enjoy her happiness; the misleading reading is that conversation is a balanced exchange of turns rather than sustained attention to her evolving inner life.
- **B:** In ten seconds on a phone, a married man would take away that his wife's present answers matter more than the answers he remembers from dating; the misleading reading is that the unexplained river and double arrow measure growing distance between partners.
- **C:** In ten seconds on a phone, a married man would take away that he should let his wife finish, respond warmly, and remember to follow up later; the misleading reading is that good conversation is mainly a listening-and-memory checklist, with his own sharing absent.

## Pick after reading the distillation

**Pick B.** It best matches “Pay attention to the people you're already becoming” by making the difference between remembered dating answers and present answers the central visual idea, while explicitly acknowledging that both people change.

**Repair for clarity:** Remove the vertical double arrow that runs through the main instruction and replace the unexplained water subtitle with “Her answer now may be different”; this preserves the changing-over-time idea without implying a measured gap between spouses.
