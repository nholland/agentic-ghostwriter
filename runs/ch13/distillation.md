# Chapter 13 Distillation — Pursue Her After You Have Her

**Mechanism:** Keep learning what matters to her

**Conversation sentence:** You can remember everything she loved when you were dating and still need to ask what she'd enjoy now.

She's still becoming. You've changed too. The answers you already had can't tell you everything about the woman beside you today. Ask what happened next. Be interested in her answer. Let yourself be pleased for her. Sustained effort means making time for those conversations and doing something with what you learn, year after year.

**Lesson:** Winning her affection begins a life of choosing her through sustained attention and effort.
**Challenge:** Familiarity makes it easy to rely on old answers; keep giving her time and attention without requiring a particular response.

**Practice:**
1. Once a week, make time together around something she enjoys now. Ask rather than relying only on what she liked when you were dating, and follow through on the plan.
2. When she tells you about something she's enjoying, set aside what you're doing and ask what caught her interest. Let yourself be pleased for her, and return to it another day.
3. When you're spending time together, tell her about something you've begun to enjoy or reconsider. Give her a chance to know who you're becoming too.
