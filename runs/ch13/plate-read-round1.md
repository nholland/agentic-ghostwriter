# Provisional plate — standalone read

**Cold read, before seeing the distillation:** Keep asking what matters to your wife now, because knowing her while dating does not mean you already know who she is becoming.

**Strongest plausible misreading:** Her priorities have become more demanding, and my job is to keep accommodating the changes. “Some things matter more to her now” invites that reading; the two parallel pairs of waves do not supply another clear meaning.

**Verdict: EDIT.** The cold takeaway matches the mechanism, “Keep learning what matters to her,” but only partly matches the conversation sentence, “Pay attention to the people you're already becoming.” The title and bold central instruction make this principally about updating my knowledge of her. My own becoming appears only in the smaller footer. Warm reception is present in “Let yourself be pleased for her,” but has less visual force than the informational task.

Necessary edits, ranked:

1. Replace the title “Keep learning what matters to her” with “Notice who you're both becoming.” This puts the intended shared becoming into the first ten-second read.
2. Replace “Some things matter more to her now” with “What matters can change for both of you.” This removes the implication that her needs have escalated and makes the mutual change explicit near the top.
3. Replace the bold “Be interested / in her answer.” with “Ask what happened next.” Move “Be interested in her answer.” directly beneath it in smaller type, and move “Let yourself be pleased for her.” immediately below that. Grouping the question and warm response makes the action legible as interested attention rather than an obligation to satisfy changing demands.

The waves look decorative rather than explanatory; they do not independently communicate change or becoming. They are not a reason by themselves to reject the plate, but the revised words must carry the meaning without assuming the waves explain it.
