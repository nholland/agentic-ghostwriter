# Ch05 Plate — The Standalone Read (stage 5)

**Desk:** Reader Panel · **Date:** 2026-09-20 22:40 UTC
**Read:** `runs/ch05/pdf/plate.png`, alone, before the distillation.

## Step 1 — cold (married man, phone, ten seconds)

**His sentence:** "If you go quiet in a fight you walk out smaller than you walked
in; if you say the real thing, you walk out the same size you came in."

**Read the right way?** Yes. The cost lands on *silence*, not on fighting. Both
rows are the same fight; the only variable is whether the box gets filled. Nothing
here says "don't argue."

**The skeptic's misreading:** A defensive man reads **GOES BACK** as *relitigating*
— she thought it was over, he drags it up again — and files the plate under
nagging. The subhead "he says the true thing" pulls against that, but only on a
second look. Second, smaller: "the real thing" is never defined on the plate; he
fills it with his own grievance rather than his own concern.

**Needs the chapter?** Only the footer. *Parrhesia* is unglossed jargon at ten
seconds — harmless as a signature line, useless as a takeaway. Everything
load-bearing is self-carrying.

## Step 2 — against the distillation

Conversation sentence: *"The fight ends before the real thing gets said. That's not
resolution. That's how a man becomes small without deciding to."*

**Verdict: PASS.** The cold sentence is that sentence in the stranger's words,
including "becomes small," arrived at without the chapter. No Ch04 fence-post or
nail imagery survives; this does not read as a repeat of the anger chapter.

**Non-blocking note for the Designer:** the GOES BACK misreading is a one-word fix
if it ever recurs — no edit requested now.
