**Raised by:** inbox #003 (gw-retro, 2026-09-13)
**Ruled by the author:** 2026-09-14 — "Approved... I think I like the latter,
keeping it separate from distillation" then "practices only"
**Targets:** the BOOK repo. This engine never writes there (CLAUDE.md Rule 8).

---

## What the ruling settles

Three artifacts covered the same ground. After this, each has one job:

| Artifact | Job | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `chapters/chNN/distillation.md` | pipeline handle: mechanism, conversation sentence, Lesson, Challenge, Practice | internal |
| `## Putting It Into Practice` | the chapter close a reader reads | end of each chapter, compiled from the distillation |
| `appendix/practice-guide.md` | the back-of-book reference | **the back of the book** (not compiled today — see change 3) |
| `tactics-review.md` | nothing. Superseded. | archived (change 1) |

The guide carries **practices only** — no Lesson, no Challenge, no summary
table. The Lesson and Challenge already land at the end of every chapter;
repeating them in the appendix would be a third copy of the chapter close. A
table is excluded because this file accumulates and a table cannot be appended
(Rule 15).

## Change 1 — archive the tactics review

```
git mv tactics-review.md archive/tactics-review-ch01-ch08.md
```

A name that states its range cannot claim to be current (Rule 15). It covered
Ch1–Ch8 while eleven chapters are refined, was hand-made 2026-08-23, and was
referenced by nothing but `.claude/LEARNINGS.md`. What it contributed — the
Proactive/Reactive tag — survives it, in change 2.

Its format was right and its wording came from the distillations, not from the
guide. It was a good artifact with no desk; now the desk exists.

## Change 2 — `/book-chapter-refine` writes the tagged format

The per-chapter section becomes:

```
## Chapter N — <Title>

1. **Proactive.** <practice>
2. **Reactive.** <practice>
3. **Reactive.** <practice>
```

**Proactive** = a standing habit or scheduled check, run on a rhythm whether or
not anything has gone wrong yet. **Reactive** = something done once a specific
moment has already started. The tag shows a reader the balance at a glance; it
does not rank them.

Two constraints on the generator:

- **The practices are the distillation's Practice items, verbatim.** Not a
  reworded second version. Measured 2026-09-14 across all eleven chapters:
  ten are already verbatim identical. Chapter 1 is the exception, and it is a
  real defect, not a paraphrase — the manuscript asks the reader *"What's this
  actually about for me right now, and how do I want to respond?"* while the
  guide asks *"Am I being the best husband I can be right now?"* Two different
  questions under one practice.
- **Append, never rewrite.** The old pipeline lost ten Practice sections once
  because a generator looked for the wrong heading and nothing errored.

## Change 3 — `/book-compile` ships the appendix

`appendix/practice-guide.md` appears nowhere in `manuscript.md`. It has been
generated by every refine and compiled into nothing. Append it after the final
chapter as the back-of-book guide, under its own heading.

This is the change that makes the ruling real. Without it the guide is still a
file nobody reads.

## Change 4 — backfill, once

Regenerate the eleven existing sections wholesale rather than editing in place:
tags added, Chapter 1's wording reconciled to its distillation. Rule 15's
"regenerated wholesale" half — the file accumulates going forward, but a format
change to existing entries is a rebuild, not eleven hand-edits.

Verify after: every guide practice byte-identical to its distillation practice,
all eleven chapters present, every item tagged.
