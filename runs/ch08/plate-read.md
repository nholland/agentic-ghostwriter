## Step 1 — cold

**Cold sentence.** Small grievances you never said out loud pile up until one
day the scale tips; the man who names each one as it happens stays level.

**Skeptic's misreading.** The right column reads as "when she complains, check
if it's true; when you do the work, expect nothing" — accept blame, forgo
credit. A defensive man sees two instructions that both cost him.

**Needs the chapter.** "the work already paid itself" is opaque cold — paid
*what*, to *whom*. Also the six underlined dots on the NAMED side: underline
reads as crossed-off/deleted, not as named-and-set-down.

**Off centre.** The left column is empty air from the dot row to the scale while
the right column is packed with two text blocks — the page reads right-heavy.
The left pan's mass sits far left of its own caption, which centres under the
fulcrum instead of under the drawing's visual weight.

## Step 2 — verdict

**EDITS.** The Conversation sentence is the left half only: unfairness arrives
as uncounted small things, tipping quietly. The plate delivers that, but hands
it over as printed text (subtitle and footer repeat it verbatim) rather than
letting the drawing carry it, and then spends the whole right column on the
Lesson — two Stoic tests the stranger has no frame for. The right side is where
both the author's "doesn't make the point clearly" and the imbalance live.

1. **Cut** "WHEN IT LANDS ON YOU / is it true, or not true?" and "WHEN IT'S
   YOURS / the work already paid itself" from the plate. They are the Lesson,
   not the Mechanism, and they import the chapter.
2. **Relabel** the right column header NAMED to "NAMED AS IT LANDS", and
   **replace** the cut text block with the practice line already in the
   distillation: "Name it to yourself right then."
3. **Replace** the underlines under the right dots with the dots falling to the
   pan singly and being drawn small — nothing accumulates. An underline reads as
   struck out.
4. **Move** the left scale up and the left dot-row down to close the empty
   middle third, so both columns have the same vertical mass.
5. **Move** the left caption to centre under the loaded pan, not the fulcrum.
6. **Cut** the footer's repeat of the subtitle; keep one, not both. Right now
   the same sentence is printed twice on one plate.

## The two pick misreadings

Misreading one ("a comment every time something bugs me") is avoided — "Name it
to yourself right then" is explicit. Misreading two is **not**: "the work
already paid itself" set directly above a level scale reads as the work zeroing
out, exactly the fear named at the pick. Edit 1 removes it.
