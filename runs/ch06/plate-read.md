# Ch06 Plate — Standalone Read (stage 5)

Desk: Reader Panel · 2026-09-20 22:00 UTC · `runs/ch06/pdf/plate.png`

## Cold, before the distillation

Married man, phone, ten seconds:

> "The week of work I actually did is solid and real; all she can see is
> tonight — and the second I say that out loud, I've started the scorekeeping."

**Skeptic's misreading (the one the drawing invites):** the empty dashed
TONIGHT box read as *her* column — "she sees nothing / does nothing tonight" —
turning two views of one man's week into a two-person comparison. Held back by
both captions being about him and his view of her, and by equal bar lengths
(same work, different visibility).

**Self-pity misread (solid = her work, "I get no credit"):** gone. The solid bar
is now his week, the subtitle denies the grievance in the first second, and the
two closing lines convert the tally from proof into the mistake.

## Against the distillation

Conversation sentence: *"Resentment doesn't come from doing too much. It comes
from being the only one who can see it."*

The cold sentence is that sentence in a stranger's words — visibility, not
volume, and the reciting is the damage.

**Verdict: PASS.**
