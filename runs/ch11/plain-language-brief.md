## Editing brief

Name the action, fear, choice, or consequence. Complete familiar expressions when their meaning depends on the missing words. State the actual distinction instead of announcing one. Give sections direct headings and make their sequence easy to follow. Preserve the author's lived facts and the chapter's intended argument. Do not fill missing autobiographical details with inventions.
