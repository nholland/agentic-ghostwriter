### Bottom line
1. **Ch16 reads in the later register, and it fixes two of the later chapters' habits.** Its beat labels ("The old picture." / "Toward her." / "Both of you." / "In flow.") describe rather than instruct, which is the fix I proposed for Q048. It also uses "a regular weekday" where earlier chapters reach for "Tuesday". It gives her a side: her own words (L21), "She looks after the place too" (L63), and what she wants (L45, L61). It makes no universal claim about men, and no line casts her as a threat.
2. **It picks up four of the later chapters' habits:**
   - a recap opening (L5-7, with meta "this chapter" lines);
   - one research-caveat sentence (L75, within the one-per-chapter limit);
   - a one-line imperative close (L99), and that close also leaves the chapter's image;
   - back-pointers instead of forward "Chapter N covers" pointers: 8 by chapter number and 10 counting the Introduction and "this book", the densest in the book.
3. **Most important cross-chapter findings:**
   - **The armor/disappearance story is now told three times** (Prologue 35-39, Ch1 79-87, Ch16 13-25). L25 reuses Ch1 85's "opt out".
   - **L37 reteaches preferred indifferents.** It repeats Ch2 50's sentence word for word: "*Indifferent* doesn't mean you don't care."
   - **Ch16 L79 treats Ch1's four questions as arête's parts.** That leaves Ch11's five qualities (Q051) contradicted by two chapters, not one.
4. **Inside the chapter, the declared single image is weakened in three ways:**
   - "place" stands in for "house" five times, which reads as a way round the cap;
   - "the places where she knows you're with her" (L59) gestures at something the sentence could name;
   - a second image, the marriage as "its own living thing, with its own health" (L85-87), competes with the house.

One check I ran against myself: Ch16 L77 says "what Chapter 1 called equanimity". That is correct; Ch1 85 defines it. L71's "In the Introduction and Part I" is also correct, because Ch2 54 has "keep flowing". Neither is a finding.

### (2) Cross-chapter patterns

- **Stock research-caveat sentence: joined once.** L75 ("Applying that to a whole marriage is my idea, not his.") has the Q053 shape, but it is the chapter's only caveat and the outline asks for it.
- **"Chapter N covers" pointers: no forward pointers.** The back-pointers are dense instead:

- **Recap opening: joined** (L5-7). The outline asks the capstone to name the arc, so this is the one recap that earns its place. The meta sentences around it do not.
- **One-line imperative ending: joined** (L99). This makes five of the last eight chapters (Q044).

**Re-used images, phrases and explanations:**
- **The armor/disappearance story.** Prologue 35-39 and Ch1 79-87 tell it. Ch1 81 already has "*I can't control her. I can only control me.*" Ch16 L17 has him say it aloud, and L25 reuses Ch1 85's "opt out". Ch2 60-66 already teaches "calm is not cold". Ch16's version is new material (said aloud, her answer), but it is the fourth time the book teaches this point.
- **Preferred indifferents.** Re-explained at L37, including Ch2 50's sentence "*Indifferent* doesn't mean you don't care." word for word.
- **Care vs. compassion.** L31 points to "Chapter 2" for feeling her mood without becoming it, but never uses Ch2's handle, "care without contagion". The mechanism is renamed (care becomes compassion) without the link being made. The outline makes compassion deliberate, but the handle should carry over.
- **Musonius.** Ch6 15 gives "community of life" glossed as "one shared project". Ch16 L49 says "one shared life". This is compatible. Ch6 15 also uses the house ("one half of the house"), which supports Ch16.
- **Going first.** Prologue 45 ("Going first… invest anyway") and Ch16 L63-65 ("You still go first") agree. The echo is good.
- **Best friend.** Prologue 11 and Ch16 L93 agree.
- **"seasons".** L51 swapped weather for "seasons", but Introduction 37 uses "seasons" in the Oak section, and so does Prologue 13. Minor.
- **"stone".** "married to a stone" (L23, the author's line) sits two paragraphs after the River is named. The Introduction 19 and Part I river "doesn't fight the stone". The meanings differ and do not contradict. No action.
- **Finance vocabulary.** "investment, not a burden" (L45) and "investing strategy" (L59) keep a finance frame alive next to Ch7's anti-ledger ("bank balance"). The outline puts *invest* inside the house family, and the author ruled. Noted only.
- **Contradicting anchors: none.** The house agrees with Introduction 47 (quoted word for word), Ch2 76 ("the foundation the warmth stands on") and Ch6 15.
- **Coinage trace: clean.** River, Oak and Sun are defined in the Introduction and the Part pages. *flow*, *arête*, *eudaimonia* and "flourishing" are new in Ch16 and glossed on first use. A grep of all chapter prose finds no earlier arête or eudaimonia, so "new to this book" is true. Equanimity is defined in Ch1 85 and Ch2 60. No undefined capitalized term.

**Early vs. late register:** clearly late. Sentences are whole and give their reasons, claims are scoped, she has a side, and the labels describe. Early habits that remain are one fragment chain (L97) and some paragraph-ending aphorisms (L41, L51 "That's what happens to a place people actually use."). One more risk: the dense back-references and repeated arc summaries flatten it toward a recap. Ch16 is the best evidence for my Part 1 claim that the later register can keep named handles ("in flow" is one) without flattening.
