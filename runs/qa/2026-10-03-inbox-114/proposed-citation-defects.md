### 14. Ch3: a barred statistic, printed, joining two different findings

*"They did it with above 90 percent accuracy, from samples as short as three
minutes."* (`chapters/ch03/refined.md` line 43)

- **What is wrong.** `okf/citations/gottman-four-horsemen.md` says the 90%+
  figure is "confirmed as unusable, not merely absent". The high figures are
  fitted and scored on the same couples; on fresh couples the hit rate falls
  to roughly 21-29% (Heyman & Slep, 2001). The sentence also joins that
  figure to a different study: the three-minute result is Carrère & Gottman
  (1999, *Family Process*, PMID 10526767), and no source found reports
  above-90% accuracy from it. The Ch3 research brief never supplied either
  number.
- **Also.** `gottman-four-horsemen.md` does not list `stop-defending-yourself`
  in `chapter_slugs`, so a slug lookup for Chapter 3 misses the concept that
  carries the bar.
- **Fix.** Cut the sentence, or replace it with a sentence that keeps the three
  minutes and drops the percentage (candidates in
  `claim-1-ch03-gottman-accuracy.md`). Add the slug. File
  `carrere-gottman-1999-first-three-minutes.md` if the three minutes stay.
- **Evidence.** PubMed record match and search-synthesis. No page opened: Wiley,
  PMC, gottman.com and the author-hosted PDF were refused by the proxy.

### 15. Ch5: the author's reading, printed as what long-married couples learned

*"They learned, over thousands of small fights, that most of them didn't need a
verdict."* (`chapters/ch05/refined.md` line 53)

- **What is wrong.** The sentence before it ("The couples who stay married for
  fifty years didn't avoid fighting") is backed at book level by
  `pillemer-long-marriages-learn-to-fight.md`. This one is not located in
  Pillemer or anywhere else. The Ch5 brief's search for an elders study saying
  "everything is small" was never closed (that phrase is Carlson's, not
  Pillemer's). The book's catalogue table of contents has no lesson about
  letting small fights go.
- **Fix.** Recast as the author's own view, or soften (candidates in
  `claim-2-ch05-fifty-years.md`). Gottman's perpetual-problems finding is the
  nearest research but makes a different claim. It is filed only as an
  unverified draft gap.
- **Evidence.** Internet Archive catalogue record (bibliographic) and
  search-synthesis. The book text, author site and Cornell page were not
  reachable.
