# Carrère & Gottman (1999): the first three minutes

## What it supports

A real study predicted divorce among newlyweds from **the first three
minutes** of a conflict discussion. That is enough to support a plain
sentence like "In one study, the first three minutes of a fight told them a
lot."

## What it does not support

- **"Above 90 percent accuracy."** No source found joins an above-90% figure to
  a three-minute sample. Chapter 3 line 43 prints that combination; see
  [Gottman — The Four Horsemen](/citations/gottman-four-horsemen.md), which
  already bars divorce-prediction percentages.
- **Any specific accuracy number** until the paper is read. Search summaries
  vary, and search fails confidently.
- Prediction for married couples in general: the sample was newlyweds.

## Status

**Verifiable**, on a bibliographic record match only. Next step for a
