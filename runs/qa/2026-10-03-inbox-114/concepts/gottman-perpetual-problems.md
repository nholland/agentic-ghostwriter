# Gottman — Perpetual Problems

## The claim, as secondary sources state it

Most of the problems couples argue about are perpetual: they come from
lasting differences in personality or needs and do not get solved. Couples who
do well keep talking about them with acceptance and humor; couples who do
badly end up gridlocked.

## Why it is here

Chapter 5 line 53 says long-married couples "learned, over thousands of small
fights, that most of them didn't need a verdict." No source for that was
found (see [Pillemer — Long Marriages Learn to Fight](/citations/pillemer-long-marriages-learn-to-fight.md)).
This is the closest real research, and it could support a softened sentence,
but only once checked.
