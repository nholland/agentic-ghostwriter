### Proposed body

Enough can be practiced by attending to the good in the actual shared life,
appreciating specific qualities and acts of the real partner, and expressing
that appreciation through words and care. The watering image points toward
where a husband puts his attention and effort. This is not a guarantee about
how she responds or a claim that every condition in the marriage is good.

Two possible practices, not a required new system: name one specific thing
about her or the shared life that has become easy to overlook; occasionally
consider how the two of you might never have met, then return attention to
what is here. Gratitude should not make the wife responsible for rewarding
his exercise. The research supports components and bounded outcomes, not the
whole moral synthesis.
