## Proposed content concept: Enough without neglect

**Decision needed from author:** Accept, correct, or reject this synthesis.
This is not in the OKF as an accepted belief. The interview establishes the
tension, but does not settle the formulation below.

**Proposed formulation:** Contentment means loving the real spouse, not making
appearance, earnings, talent, or another person's best trait into a condition
on her worth. Complacency means letting that security become an excuse to stop
attending to her, tending one's own health, or speaking honestly about a
genuine shared concern. “Enough” is a commitment to the person, not a claim
that every circumstance is ideal. Wisdom asks whether a concern involves
shared care or a preference being promoted into a demand. The chapter should
not pretend there is a fixed threshold that answers every couple's case.

**Grounding:** Direct author words in `interview.md`, especially “You still
need to maintain your health,” “She is enough for me,” and “I don't know the
clear line”; source findings in `research.md`. Research supports pieces of
the distinction; the moral meaning and wording are the author's decision.
