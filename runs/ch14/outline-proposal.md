# Chapter 14 outline revision proposal

Status: proposed; do not edit `03-outline.md` until author approves.
Authority: Chapter 14 interview, 2026-09-27. This is a proposal for the
constitution, not an accepted content concept.

```diff
- **Premise:** The comparison a man loses to is never a real rival — it's an imagined composite built from other people's best single traits, and no real wife, or real husband, can survive being measured against a highlight reel.
+ **Premise:** A husband learns to love the real person his wife becomes across years of change. Contentment keeps beauty, income, talent, and passing comparisons from becoming conditions on his love, while wisdom still asks which genuine concerns deserve a conversation.
- **The reader's takeaway:** He can name the specific mechanism that turns a stranger's one striking trait into apparent proof his wife isn't enough, and has a standard — contentment as a chosen, practiced discipline, not a feeling he's waiting to arrive — for what "enough" actually means in the marriage he already chose.
+ **The reader's takeaway:** He can notice when a preference has become a demand his wife must satisfy to be loved, practice contentment with her changing self, and speak honestly about a genuine concern without making her worth depend on its outcome.
- **Central story/example:** A man on a work trip who can't stop thinking about a colleague's easy laugh and effortless confidence — and, that same week, a conversation with his wife where he realizes she could build an equally damning highlight reel about some other man if she let herself, and neither of them would survive that game, so neither of them should play it
+ **Central story/example:** The author's real account of his wife as a light sleeper, morning person, frugal keeper of small things, mother on sabbatical after an executive career, gardener, and woman whose new gray hairs he loves. Use only details that survive author review; do not invent dialogue. The work-trip comparison may remain a clearly fictional secondary example if useful.
- **Reader ah-ha:** "I wasn't comparing her to a person. I was comparing her to a highlight reel — and so could she, about me, if she let herself."
+ **Reader ah-ha:** "I can want us both to care for ourselves and still love the people we are becoming. A preference is not a condition she has to meet to be enough."
```

The old comparison and proactive-practice points can remain as subordinate
mechanisms. The research pass must test, rather than assume, the empirical
claims about aging, attraction, and media-shaped expectations. The exact
wisdom test for whether a concern deserves a conversation remains unresolved
by the interview and is not invented here.
