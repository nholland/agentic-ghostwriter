# Source finding and limits

Direct Gummere transcription inspected. Section 7 says Epicurus addressed Idomeneus about making Pythocles rich through reducing desires; translator note 5 locates Epicurus fragment 135 Usener. Section 8 extends the thought beyond money. Bertolotti substitutes a generic man and shortens the sentence: paraphrase, not house-edition wording.

Attribute to Epicurus as quoted by Seneca, not a surviving letter written to Pythocles and not a distinctively Stoic discovery. It provides a second philosophical route into enough but overlaps Letters 2 and 16; select one rather than stacking all. No empirical finding about marriage or income follows.
