# Source finding

As the abstract is reported (PubMed record surfaced in search): maximization correlated positively with regret and depression and negatively with happiness, optimism, self-esteem and life satisfaction; maximizers were less satisfied with consumer decisions, compared themselves with others more, and were more affected by upward comparison.

# Limits

Correlational; consumer and general decisions, not partner choice. 'Satisficing' means choosing what is good enough by one's standards, not settling for less in a wife; do not frame it that way.
