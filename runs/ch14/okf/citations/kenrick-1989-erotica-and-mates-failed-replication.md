# Source finding

The 1989 Study 2 reported that men exposed to nude centerfolds rated their partners as less attractive and reported less love. Three high-powered replications found no such effect for men or women; the replication authors suggested the original's small sample inflated its effects.

# Limits

One narrow paradigm (brief exposure, immediate ratings). It neither establishes nor rules out longer-term effects of pornography. It does remove the classic experimental support for 'porn makes men want less of their wives.' The canonical pornography/romance-media gap stays open.
