# Source finding

An original study and a conceptual replication with independent couple samples (combined n reported as 370): the expresser's other-praising behavior was linked to the benefactor seeing the expresser as responsive, to positive feelings and to feeling loving; alternative explanations were tested.

# Limits

Couples, not necessarily married. Supports the chapter's existing 'tell her what you saw and why it mattered'; adds no new direction. Algoe's broader find-remind-bind theory (Algoe 2012, Social and Personality Psychology Compass 6(6), 455-469, a review) frames gratitude as binding people to responsive partners; theory only.
