# Source finding and limits

Gap marker. Bertolotti attributes a few-wants definition of wealth to Epictetus without a work or section. Search yielded repeated and inconsistent attributions, no traceable ancient locator. No reliable conclusion that the line is authentic or definitively fabricated is available. Do not print it as an Epictetus quotation.

A thematically related authentic passage is Enchiridion 39.1, directly inspected in Greek with Albert Watanabe’s commentary at https://dcc.dickinson.edu/epictetus-encheiridion/chapter-39 . It measures possessions by bodily needs and warns that going beyond a limit has no end. That passage does not establish the popular sentence’s wording or provenance. Dickinson is not the house Long translation: no English quotation is commissioned from it. Its shoe/cliff imagery should remain off the chapter’s watering stage.
