# Source finding

As the abstract and an APA press release are reported in search: people on average reacted to marital transitions and then moved back toward baseline life satisfaction, but individual differences were substantial; some remained far from baseline years later.

# Limits

Measures life satisfaction, not marital satisfaction or love. German panel. The 'one tenth of a point' figure is from a press release; omit. Supports 'the lift of a good change fades on average'; does not support 'the honeymoon always ends' or any universal courtship-chemistry claim (already retired from Ch14's outline).
