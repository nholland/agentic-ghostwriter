# Source finding

As the abstract is reported in university repository listings surfaced by search: Study 1 found dispositionally grateful people form more favorable benefit appraisals, which fully accounted for the link between trait and state gratitude; Study 2 replicated this with daily reports of real events; Study 3 showed benefit appraisals causally shaped state gratitude and mediated the effect of the situation on it.

# Limits

Not about marriage. Experimental evidence is for appraisals of described help scenarios, not spouses. Supports the general mechanism the author describes (seeing the cost changes the gratitude), not his specific route of doing the work oneself.
