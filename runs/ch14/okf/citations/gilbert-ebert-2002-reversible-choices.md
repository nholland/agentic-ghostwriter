# Source finding

Having the opportunity to change one's mind reduced liking for the chosen print, apparently by disrupting the post-decision processes that increase satisfaction; participants did not predict this, and most wanted the reversible option.

# Limits

Prints, not people. Any extension to marriage ('keeping your options open in your head makes you value her less') is an extrapolation the author must own. Do not present it as a marriage finding.
