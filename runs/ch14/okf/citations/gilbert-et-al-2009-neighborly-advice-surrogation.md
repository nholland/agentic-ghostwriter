# Source finding

As the abstract is reported (Harvard DASH record in search): undergraduates predicted their reactions to a five-minute speed date (n = 25) and a peer evaluation (n = 88) more accurately when told only how another person had reacted than when given information about the event; participants and judges expected the opposite.

# Limits

Short events, students. Supports 'imagination is a weak guide to an experience you have not had' (the author's 'horizon limitations'). Does not show imagined partners disappoint or that marriages are better than alternatives.
