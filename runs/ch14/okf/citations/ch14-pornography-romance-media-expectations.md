# Research question

Does exposure to pornography or romantic fiction influence expectations of
partner appearance, sexual experience, or partner behavior? Treat each medium
and outcome separately; the interview names possibilities, not evidence.

# Partial finding, 2026-10-19

The classic experimental claim, Kenrick, Gutierres & Goldberg (1989) Study 2,
was that men rated their partners less attractive and reported less love after
seeing centerfolds. Three higher-powered replications by Balzarini et al.
(JESP, 2016 online / 2017) did not reproduce it. This removes the best-known
experimental support for "porn makes men value their wives less." It neither
establishes nor rules out longer-term effects. See
`kenrick-1989-erotica-and-mates-failed-replication.md` (staged alongside).
