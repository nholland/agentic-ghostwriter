# Source finding

Long's Book VI passage conventionally numbered 48 recommends recalling the good qualities of people with whom one lives as a source of delight, and keeping those qualities in view. It gives examples of activity, modesty, and generosity. This supplies a positive practice of attention, not merely resistance to desire.

# Application limit

Philosophical counsel about nearby people, not a marriage study. Applying it to a wife's character is an editorial application. It does not require declaring every irritating habit a virtue. Online Long text inspected; no physical-edition check.

# Candidate quotation for Chapter 14

Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* VI.48, George Long translation: “When thou
wishest to delight thyself, think of the virtues of those who live with thee”.

point, not a requirement that the chapter quote archaic diction. The author
