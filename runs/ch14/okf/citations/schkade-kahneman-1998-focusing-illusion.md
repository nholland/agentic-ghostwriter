# Source finding

Self-reported life satisfaction did not differ between regions, but people rating a similar other in the other region predicted Californians would be more satisfied; climate and cultural-opportunity satisfaction carried the predicted difference. Easily observed, distinctive differences get more weight in such judgments than they have in lived experience.

# Limits

Not about relationships. Applying it to an imagined other partner ('you judge her by the one thing you can see') is the author's application. Economists have offered alternative explanations of the regional pattern (secondary blog); not a defect in the finding as stated.
