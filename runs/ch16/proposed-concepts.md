title: 'Self-Command, Not Detachment: What Stoicism Gave Him and How He Overused It'

  steadiness aimed at her good, not away from her.

Body (his words, in order):
- "it put a label on the fact that being angry was a state of temporary madness. You were effectively out of control and a man who is powerful and strong is in control. A man who is effectively slavish to his emotions is weak"
- "It was also fascinating that I don't have to give in to the emotions of a situation. When someone is upset I am able to remain calm and balanced."
- "I think what Stoicism gives you are tools on how to navigate and to not be a slave to your emotions."
- "Stoicism gets a bad rap because a lot of times people can seem like they are unemotional or detached and I think that is a sign of immaturity in the study of the philosophy."
