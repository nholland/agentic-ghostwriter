# Ch15 plate: standalone read (e)

Reader Panel, cold read 2026-10-25. Image only: runs/ch15/pdf/plate.png. I did not read the chapter, the distillation or any notes.

## 1. What he takes away (written before reading the target)

"When her day dips into hostility, frustration or coldness, I'm the steady line underneath pushing it back up with kindness, patience and warmth, and at the end of the day I ask whether I was the best part of it."

## 2. Against the target

Target: "When your wife's day turns sharp, meet hostility with kindness, frustration with patience, and disconnection with warmth, and ask yourself each day whether you were the best part of her day."

What landed:
- All three pairings, in order. The header states them and the columns repeat them, so they can't be missed.
- The "when her day turns sharp" trigger. The V-shaped dips in a line labeled HER DAY carry it without the subtitle.
- The daily question. It is set apart at the bottom and reads as the thing to keep.

What didn't come through:
- "Each day" is weak. The question reads as a slogan more than a nightly habit, but the quotation marks and "I" do make it his own question. A small loss.

Verdict: PASS. My sentence is the takeaway.

## 3. What confuses or misleads (max three)

1. **"let it go" can read as said to her.** A defensive man, or his wife looking over his shoulder, can hear "just let it go" said to her, which is dismissive. Read his way, it sounds passive: swallow it. It is the weakest of the three sublabels.
2. **The arrows read as support, not cause.** They rise from YOU and stop at the bottom of each dip, so they hold her up. But YOU is a flat, untouched line and every dip is hers, so the skeptic's reading is "her feelings are defects and fixing them is my job." The drawing invites the "manage her moods" reading a little. It doesn't force it.
3. **"start helping first" is vague in ten seconds.** First before what: before she asks, or before I get defensive? It doesn't obviously spell out "patience" the way "let her talk" spells out "warmth."
