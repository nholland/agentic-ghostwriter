## Gaps

### 1. Where his account ends and the reader's composite begins, and what the composite may contain
- **What is missing.** The brief says "no detail may be added" to the pattern (A8, C), "no setting detail"
  (C), and "first person for his account, then back to 'you'" (C). It never says what the "you" turns may
  contain. The section 0 felt moment ("a small sharp remark lands and the reply is already in his mouth")
  and the return to "you" after each first-person piece in section 2 are reader composites. Under
  `04-archetype.md` composites are the book's primary evidence, and `01-voice.md` asks for "specific,
  named detail." Two rules pull against each other here and nothing settles which one wins. Smaller
  points from the same gap: is the tantrum (D27) one of his children or a general example? And
  is he tired on the Saturday-morning version? D22's tiredness is tied to "I would come in." The license
  to carry it over is "It's the same exact scenario, just not in the evening," which is in the story
  concept but not in D21.
- **Where it belonged.** `research.md` C, "Central story rules", as one more rule.
- **What I would be tempted to invent.** For the reader's Saturday, a list of errands, somewhere to sit that
  is not the couch, a time on the clock, a child's age. "When my youngest is hungry" for the tantrum. A
  reason he is tired on a Saturday ("after a long week").
- **Who can close it.** A cold desk (Publisher or Researcher). For example: reader turns restate only the
  given inventory in the second person, with generic placeholders such as "the list," "the weekend," "a
  child," and with no possessive that makes them his; the tantrum is a general example; add the
  "same exact scenario" sentence to D21.

### 2. Section 1 asks for a staged, real-time confession, and the record has no moment to stage
- **What is missing.** Section 1 says "His confession, staged as the version of him about to get it wrong."
  That is `01-voice.md`'s "Stage your own mistakes in real time... show the moment before you knew better."
  But the record has no dated moment (A8), no reply he gave (C: "No reply he gave in the years he
  retaliated"), no length of time ("a growing trend," A3, with no span), and no turning point (D24: "I
  realized over time"). The brief does not say how to stage it with only what was given, or that the
  change must be told as gradual with no event that set it off.
- **Where it belonged.** `research.md` C, the section 1 row, or a line in the central-story rules.
- **What I would be tempted to invent.** A remembered retort ("I'd fire back with..."). "For years." "When the
  kids were small." A morning when he noticed. The pressure is strongest because the voice rule demands
  the moment and section 1 is 200-230 words of confession.
- **Who can close it.** A cold desk: say that section 1 stages him through the interior he gave (tired,
  frustrated that "immediately she was giving me things to do," hearing "lazy," D22 and D24), told as a
  pattern; that the change is "over time" and "learned through experience" with no trigger; and that the
  staged-in-real-time rule is met by his interior, not by an incident. Only the author could supply an
  actual moment, and he has said he can't.

### 3. The image budget does not add up, and the chapter has three candidate images
- **What is missing.** H caps the game family at "about 4-5 total, including the label," and counts "game."
  The section 3 label "**Chess, not checkers.**" is 2 mentions. The spec's required sentence "Checkers is
  answering every exchange; chess is the long game" is 3 more. That makes 5, at the ceiling, before D10/D11's
  "long game" context, any later reference, or a close in "the chapter's own image" (`01-voice.md` Never Do,
  on wrapping up). Then there is a second spatial image: floor and ceiling. The author chose it as the spine,
  and the spec prints it in the premise and in key points 1 and 4. The brief never counts it or says
  whether "floor" and "ceiling" appear in the prose. H allows a third, "sun," as one declared mention.
  `01-voice.md` says "One concrete image per chapter, max." The brief also never rules on "pour kindness
  into my wife" (D10) as a liquid image next to the Ch14 water fence.
- **Where it belonged.** `research.md` H, "Image family cap", and G, "Images."
- **What I would be tempted to do.** Quietly leave "long game" or floor/ceiling out of the count, or declare
  a 5-cap family and then break it in the close.
- **Who can close it.** A cold desk. Rule on: (a) which family is declared; (b) whether "game" in "long
  game" counts; (c) whether the label counts, or whether to relabel section 3 (for example "**The long
  game.**") to free two mentions; (d) whether floor and ceiling are printed words or only structure (if
  printed, how they are counted next to chess); (e) whether the close may use the anchor image.
