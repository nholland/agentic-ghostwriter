What confuses or reads wrong:

1. **"ZING" (title and subtitle).** Slang that nothing on the plate defines. I guessed it means a jab or dig, but the whole plate depends on that guess. Either define it or use a word a stranger already knows.
2. **"an equal or escalated response."** It sits under all four top arrows, so it reads as labelling the whole exchange. Attach it to the two YOU-to-HER arrows (the dark, growing ones) so it is clearly *his* move.
3. **Unlabelled divider.** Nothing tells me that the bottom half replaces the top half. I only worked that out from the footer. The line needs a word (e.g. "instead") or the halves need labels.
4. **"how she's feeling on the inside" over the bold zigzag.** I can't tell whether it relabels her zing (the zing *is* her feelings) or shows a second thing she sends. It is also drawn in black, the heaviest line on the plate, so her zing is what the eye lands on in the "good" half. Make it clear the label names the arrow, and lighten the line.
5. **Skeptic's misreading.** Every zing starts at HER, and the payoff for taking it is doing "a chore that's usually hers." A defensive man reads this as "she snipes, I do her housework," and "usually hers" sounds like the plate approves of that division of chores. Relabel the box so it doesn't say whose chore it is.
6. **"THE BEST PART OF HER DAY."** This overclaims for one chore. To a skeptic it reads as sarcasm or a promise the plate can't keep, so it needs softer, smaller wording.
