# Ch15 plate pick (gw-panel, cold)

Read: the three PNGs only. Nothing else in runs/ch15 was opened. One caveat: the Conversation sentence was in the same dispatch message, below a line. I wrote Step 1 from the images before working with it, but I had seen it.

## Step 1: the ten-second read

**A (HER / YOU, zigzags):** "When she zings me, don't zing back, because that is how fights escalate. Her zing is how she's feeling, so start helping instead." *Confusing:* the largest zigzag, the third one, goes HER to YOU, so the escalation is drawn as hers. The caption "an equal or escalated response" sits under all three lines, so it isn't clear which ones are his.

**B (stack of boxes):** "She snaps because she's carrying too much. Take one of her chores and do it gladly." *Confusing:* the dashed box. Is it the overflow that causes the zing, or the box that moves to him? The arrow starts from it and the zing comes out of it. His box is also grey while hers are white, so it doesn't read as the same chore.

**C (checkers / chess):** "Trading jabs is checkers. Play the long game and aim to be the best part of her day." *Confusing:* chess is played against an opponent, so the wife becomes his opponent. A pawn marching to a target square is hard to read as anything else. Nothing in it shows what he should actually do.

## Step 2: the pick

**Pick: A.** His ten-second sentence ("don't zing back, it's how she feels, start helping") is the Conversation sentence in his own words. It is the only concept that puts help *before the answer* ("Roll up your sleeves before you open your mouth").

**Runner-up: B.** It is the clearest picture of what helping looks like, and its subtitle asks "how she feels." It loses because it never shows the reply he holds back, so "before you answer" is missing. The skeptic also reads it as blame: "she's sharp because I don't do my share."

**All three miss part of the sentence:** none lands all three parts. "Best part of her day" appears only in C.

## Weaknesses in A for the Designer

1. **Skeptic misreading.** The biggest zigzag is hers, so a defensive man reads "she escalates, I'm the victim." Make the return zigzags (YOU to HER) the ones that grow, and attach "an equal or escalated response" to those lines only.
2. Attach "how she's feeling on the inside" directly to her zing, for example "her zing = how she's feeling on the inside," so it doesn't float between the two halves.
3. The help arrow has nothing on it. Put one small chore box riding the curved arrow, taken from B.
4. "Best part of her day" is missing. Add it as the final caption line, or as the label where the help arrow lands on HER.
