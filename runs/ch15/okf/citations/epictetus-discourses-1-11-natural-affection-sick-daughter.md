# Epictetus, Discourses I.11 (Long): leaving a sick child is not affection

**What it says, plainly:** Being so overwhelmed by someone's suffering that you leave is not love.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> little daughter was sick … I could not endure to stay with her, but I left home till a person sent me news that she had recovered.

> to leave your sick child and to go away is not reasonable

> it results that your behavior was not at all an affectionate act.
