# Seneca, De Beneficiis II.1-2 (Stewart): give willingly, quickly; anticipate

**What it says, plainly:** Don't wait to be asked. Give fast, give gladly, and say something kind while you do it.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> II.1: let us give in the way in which we ourselves should like to receive. Above all we should give willingly, quickly, and without any hesitation

> II.1: The best thing of all is to anticipate a person's wishes

> II.2: however quick he may be, a man gives too late who gives what he has been asked for.

> Adjacent, II.3: they say 'yes' with a face which seems to say 'no.' How much better is it to join kind words to kind actions
