# Seneca, Letter 48.2 (Gummere): live for your neighbour

**What it says, plainly:** A life aimed only at your own advantage does not work, even for you.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> no one can live happily who has regard to himself alone and transforms everything into a question of his own utility; you must live for your neighbour, if you would live for yourself.
