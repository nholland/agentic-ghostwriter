# Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VII.74 (Long): do not be tired of receiving what is useful

**What it says, plainly:** Doing good for others is itself the benefit, so there is no reason to tire of it.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> No man is tired of receiving what is useful. But it is useful to act according to nature. Do not then be tired of receiving what is useful by doing it to others.
