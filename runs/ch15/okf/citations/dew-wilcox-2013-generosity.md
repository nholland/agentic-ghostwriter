# Dew & Wilcox (2013): generosity and marital quality

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Generosity, defined as small acts of kindness, respect, affection and forgiving faults, went with higher satisfaction and lower conflict and perceived divorce likelihood. A secondary report adds that couples where only one spouse reported high generosity fared worse (unconfirmed).
