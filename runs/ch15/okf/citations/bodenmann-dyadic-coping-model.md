# Bodenmann's dyadic coping (systemic-transactional model)

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Stress communication is necessary but not sufficient; the partner must perceive and interpret it, then respond. Sources disagree on whether taking over tasks is 'supportive' or 'delegated'; the distinction is timing (on request versus offered ahead).
