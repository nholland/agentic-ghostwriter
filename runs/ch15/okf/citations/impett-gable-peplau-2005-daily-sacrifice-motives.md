# Impett, Gable & Peplau (2005): daily sacrifice for approach versus avoidance motives

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Sacrifices made for approach reasons went with better daily emotion and relationship quality, and with still being together later. Sacrifices made for avoidance reasons went with worse days and more breakups.
