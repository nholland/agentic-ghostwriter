# Reis, Maniaci & Rogge (2017): compassionate acts and daily well-being among newlyweds

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Compassionate acts were associated with better emotional days for both giver and recipient. The giver's benefit was larger and did not depend on the act being noticed; the recipient's benefit depended on noticing it.

**Evidence label:** SEARCH ONLY (section A1 of the research file). Nobody opened the paper.
