# Seneca, De Beneficiis I.6 (Stewart): the spirit, not the thing given

**What it says, plainly:** The same act can be kind or not; the spirit decides.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> What, then, is a benefit? It is the art of doing a kindness which both bestows pleasure and gains it by bestowing it … It is not, therefore, the thing which is done or given, but the spirit in which it is done or given, that must be considered
