# Seneca, De Beneficiis I.1 (Stewart): a benefit given out of weariness

**What it says, plainly:** Help given grudgingly, just to end the friction, lands as grudging.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> Who can be grateful for what has been disdainfully flung to him, or angrily cast at him, or been given him out of weariness, to avoid further trouble?

> A benefit is received in the same temper in which it is given
