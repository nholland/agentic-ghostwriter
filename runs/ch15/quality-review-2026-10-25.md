## The structural problem: the chapter lost your thesis

Your own words, Round 2: *"I think the point of the chapter is that you have to be strong enough to be the best husband you can be at all times... irrespective of what the other person is doing."*

That sentence isn't in the chapter. Neither are the lines that make it strength rather than surrender:

- *"Frankly, neither was she. But that's the point, who has the strength to de-escalate?"* The chapter kept the question but cut "neither was she", so it reads as her attacking and you absorbing it.
- *"Somebody has to be calm. Somebody has to be patient. Somebody has to be the one that brings the warmth..."* This is the emotional peak of your interview, and it's missing.
- *"I think a lot of men, if you asked them, 'Are you the best part of your wife's day?', would say no."* This is the hook to the ceiling, and it's missing.
- *"Arguing styles drift over time, likely like evolution. You use what rewards."* The chapter kept the last four words without the first sentence that explains them. That's why every reader stumbled on it, and why #159 asks you something you already answered.
- *"I was fed up with how we argued."* The irony that started the chapter is gone.

Without these, the Reader Panel's skeptic keeps landing on the same misreading: "she snipes, and my answer is to do her chores." The house tried to patch that with "None of this is surrender" and the cruel-remarks line. Your own words already answer it, and better: you hold the higher line because you are the strong one, not because she's right.
