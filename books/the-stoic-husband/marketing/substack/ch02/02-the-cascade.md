The cascade doesn't start where it ends. It starts with you, at the door, carrying something the house didn't cause.

You've had the kind of day you can feel in your shoulders. You walk in, she says something, it lands wrong, you snap. Nothing dramatic. Just edges. She carries that into the kitchen where the kids already are. The tone she brings them isn't the one she started the day with. The kids catch it. Pass it. The youngest, running out of targets, kicks the dog.

The dog did nothing.

One ungoverned arrival. An entire household in distress. Ending with an animal that had no part in any of it. Nobody decided to pass it along. It ran on its own until it ran out of people.

This is different from catching her mood. There, you're the receiver. Here, you're the source. Most men never make that connection because the damage doesn't show up where they are. It shows up at the end of the line.

Marcus Aurelius called the governing part of a man the *hegemonikon*, the ruling faculty. Not the part that reacts. The part that decides. *"The ruling Reason it is that can arouse and deflect itself, make itself whatever it will, and invest everything that befalls with such a semblance as it wills."*

Translation: that part of you can spin up or settle down. It can decide what the day meant before you walk in. It can reframe what you're carrying. It isn't at the mercy of the commute. And neither are you.

You have a ruling faculty. It applies to what you carry through the door just as much as what you do once you're inside.

The backward trace is how you start to use it. When the evening has gone sideways and the whole house feels off, don't try to fix it in the moment. Trace it back. What time did the room turn? Who was in it? Where did that person's state come from? Follow the chain. It almost always leads back to one arrival, one moment at the door.

That trace isn't blame. Nobody in it did anything wrong. The cascade doesn't need bad intentions. It needs undefended people. The trace is information: this is where the chain started, this is how far it ran. Once you can see that, you start catching it at the door. The hegemonikon, deployed before you cross the threshold: what am I carrying, and is this what I'm choosing to bring in?

The dog's fine. But he shouldn't have been part of your commute.
