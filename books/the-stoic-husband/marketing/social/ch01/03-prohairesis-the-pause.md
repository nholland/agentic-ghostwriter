## LinkedIn
A former slave who became one of history's most quoted philosophers had a simple two-step practice for the moment right before you say something you'll regret: notice the feeling without acting on it, then ask what you actually want to do with it. He called the second part *prohairesis*, your faculty of choice. Most men skip straight to reacting and never know the choice point was even there.
