## LinkedIn
Most men replay an argument asking who was right. There's a better question, and it has nothing to do with her: was I being the best husband I could be tonight? Four specific checks, not a feeling: logical, kind, self-controlled, brave enough to stay engaged. The replay doesn't fix the fight. It trains the next one.
