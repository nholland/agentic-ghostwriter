1. The reader is a live case of exactly the reader-in-crisis this book is
for: mid-rough-patch, actively trying to self-regulate, asking for a tool
rather than a belief.

states the Three-Second Window/Virtue Question is meant to "return
throughout as the book's reflective anchor for every scenario." Chapter
8's "When it's yours" beat is a scenario where that anchor does not
currently appear — the fix this signal points to is not a new invented
tool, it's applying the book's own established one where the chapter is
currently reframe-only.
