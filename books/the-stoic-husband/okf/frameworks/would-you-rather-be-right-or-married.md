# Would You Rather Be Right or Be Married?

The author's saying, in his words: "Would you rather be right? Or would you rather
be married?" He says it to people and teaches it to his sons.

# Origin

No origin could be established (Researcher, runs/ch15/research-premise.md, 2026-10-23).
A journalist reports hearing a version as pre-wedding advice with no originator named,
and a neighboring phrase, "right or happy," is popularly traced to *A Course in Miracles*.
Neither was confirmed. Treat as a saying the author uses. Do not call it an old saying
or attribute it to anyone.
