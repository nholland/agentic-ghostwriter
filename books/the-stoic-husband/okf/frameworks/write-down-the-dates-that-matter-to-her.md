# Write Down the Dates That Matter to Her

In the author's words: "I think we over-index on little acts of service. I think that the other things that you are investing in there are like remembering dates. It might be hard for men to remember anniversaries. It might be hard for men to remember special things but in today's technology world what you do need to do is just have a system for putting it down somewhere. Just jot down dates that are important to her."

"The reality is that if you haven't done that then it shows that you haven't really thought about your investing strategy. That's why I would say that is an area where it's not just acts of service. It's about showing, it's like places where you show up and she knows that you are investing in the relationship. You are with her."

"There are multiple times where the wife wants to feel like she's a priority and it's not a controlling factor. It's just that she wants to know that you are invested in the relationship. It's not that she's a passenger or that it's just two separate people cohabitating."
