# Virtuous vs. Performative Romance

**Performative** = doing romantic things to get a response (sex, admiration,
reassurance, fear of her leaving).

**Virtuous** = doing romantic things because love deserves expression and you
refuse to let familiarity become neglect.

External behavior may be identical. The inner source — anxiety vs. character —
is the Stoic line.
