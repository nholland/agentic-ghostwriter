# The Marriage in Flow

In the author's words: "the aha should be treating the marriage as a joint unit that you continually nurture and that you want to exist in a point of equanimity. You want the marriage to reach its highest potential, arete, and you are trying to ultimately find eudaimonia for the marriage."

"there's a larger concept that is popular in business that people call flow. ... Flow is effectively when everything is going well: you're at peace and you're operating at your highest self."

"when you, as a husband, are doing a great job delivering warmth, love, joy, and reciprocation in the marriage, you will start to see that everything starts to move into flow. Again the three Stoic words are representative of flow."

"this warmth chapter should be the capstone that says: when you do all this, when you're strong, when you're calm, and when you're loving, everything culminates in their marriage being in flow"

Tone (same round): a Sun chapter about warmth and joy; "spending too much time in this chapter on when I use stoicism to become cold could miss the entire point of the sun arc".
