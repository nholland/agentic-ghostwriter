# Defending Mom's Standing in the House

The author holds two related boundaries with his kids, both enforced
directly by him as the household's boundary-setter: (1) when mom asks for
something and it doesn't get done, that's named as subversion and
disrespect, not just a missed chore; (2) there is no raised voice and no
physical aggression toward her — a firm line given that his own kids are
large and physically intimidating. The governing principle he names: the
golden rule (treat others as you want to be treated), followed by "respect
your elders" — and he's explicit that establishing and holding the
enforcement itself is his job, not something that manages itself.
