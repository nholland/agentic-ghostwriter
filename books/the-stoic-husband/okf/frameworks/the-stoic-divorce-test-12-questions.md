# The Stoic Divorce Test (12 Questions)

Twelve questions for evaluating whether to stay or leave, culminating in:

- Would staying be courage or fear?
- Would leaving be wisdom or escape?

# Key Insight

*The action itself does not reveal the virtue. The motive and judgment do.*
