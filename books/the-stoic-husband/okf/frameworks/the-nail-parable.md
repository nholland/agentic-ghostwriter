# The Nail Parable

A parable widely circulated but of unclear original attribution (sometimes
"Cherokee legend," sometimes anonymous). Each anger incident drives a nail into
the wood. When you calm down, you pull the nail out. The hole stays. Over time
the wood is marked with holes you can no longer trace to specific moments.

# Schema

- **Use:** Visual anchor for the accumulation claim in Ch 4. "You can pull out
  every nail you ever drove. The holes don't fill."
- **Attribution note:** Do not attribute to a specific tradition without
  verification. Use as an unnamed parable.
