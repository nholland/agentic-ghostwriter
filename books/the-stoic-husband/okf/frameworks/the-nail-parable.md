# The Nail Parable

A parable widely circulated but of unclear original attribution (sometimes
"Cherokee legend," sometimes anonymous). Each anger incident drives a nail into
the wood. An apology pulls the nail out. The hole stays. Over time
the wood is marked with holes you can no longer trace to specific moments.

# Schema

- **Use:** Visual anchor for the accumulation claim in Ch 4. "You can pull out
  every nail you ever drove. The holes don't fill."
- **Attribution note:** Do not attribute to a specific tradition without
  verification. Use as an unnamed parable.

# Placement

# Reconciliation 2026-09-27

Approved mapping: anger incident = nail; apology = removing the nail; hole =
remaining damage. The Chapter 4 caption “It's full of holes.” emphasizes accumulation.
Chapter 5 calls unresolved hurts unresolved hurts, not remaining nails. Its mechanism
is “The courage to come back”: ending a fight does not complete the repair. The
earlier calming-down mapping is superseded by this approved manuscript usage. Authority and correction history: [approved inbox decisions](/notes/2026-09-27-approved-inbox-knowledge.md).
