# Biological Mate-Seeking → Marriage Complacency

Evolutionary biology invests an enormous amount of cognitive and emotional
energy in the mate-seeking phase. Aesthetics matter. Courtship triggers daily
reflection: *What does she like? What did I do wrong? How do I show up better
tomorrow?*

The married man stops this naturally — the biological mandate has been
satisfied. The energy redirects to careers, children, and external achievement.
The marriage gets taken for granted — not from cruelty, but from biology.

The man who goes to bed single every night is structurally incentivized to
reflect and adjust. The married man must *choose* to hold himself to that
standard without the biological urgency. The three-second gap, the evening
review, the daily practice — these are the infrastructure the married man has to
build deliberately to replace what courtship provided automatically.

> "If you went to bed every night single, you would reflect on the things you
> need to do to get a spouse. When you're married, you stop adjusting because
> you've already locked it down."

> "The 3-second gap is really an in-the-moment control mechanism that is
> ultimately built brick by brick through reflection and practice. The man who
> never decides that he had a poor reaction will never build the awareness to
> execute in the gap."

# Schema

| Phase | Biological state | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Mate-seeking (single / courtship) | Mandate unsatisfied; energy deployed toward securing a mate | Daily reflection and adjustment: what does she like, what did I do wrong, how do I improve |
| Married | Mandate satisfied; energy redirects to careers, children, external achievement | Reflection and adjustment stop by default; marriage taken for granted |

Marriage complacency is the biological default, not a character flaw. The
married man must deliberately build the infrastructure — the three-second gap,
the evening review, the daily practice — to replace what courtship provided for
free.
