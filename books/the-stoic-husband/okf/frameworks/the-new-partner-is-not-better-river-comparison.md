# The New Partner Is Not Better (The River Comparison)

Divorced people who fall hard for a new partner are often comparing apples to
oranges. The new partner isn't better — the *circumstances* are completely
different: no children together, no shared financial hardship, young impulses
that led to cheating have settled into middle-age, stressful family situations
have faded as people mature and parents pass, and the people themselves are
older and wiser. "No man steps in the same river twice" — the divorced person is
not the same person who married the first time, and neither is the marriage.

# Schema

- **Key insight:** It is unfair to say the new partner is better than the old
  one. The baggage is gone — not just the emotional holes from anger, but the
  entire accumulated weight of a full shared life: financial strain, parenting
  exhaustion, family conflict, youthful mistakes. Those are circumstantial, not
  personal.
- **Implication:** The marriage you are in right now is also not being evaluated
  fairly — it is being compared against an imaginary version that doesn't carry
  the real weight of a real life together.
