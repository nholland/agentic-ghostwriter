# Daily Stoic Marriage Practices

A five-practice framework for the Stoic husband's day:

1. **Morning forethought** (Marcus) — before contact, anticipate difficulty,
   commit to affection and justice.
2. **Pre-conflict pause** (Epictetus) — when triggered, identify the
   appearance before acting.
3. **Midday act of care** (Marcus/Musonius) — one small unsolicited act, text,
   touch, or gratitude statement.
4. **Weekly household council** (Musonius) — review money, schedules,
   intimacy, children, stress.
5. **Evening self-examination** (Seneca) — where did I assent too quickly?
   Where was I vain or unjust? What repair do I owe?
