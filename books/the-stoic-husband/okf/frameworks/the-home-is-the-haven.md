# The Home Is the Haven

The author (Round 4): "I think people want to be chosen, and they want to feel safe. They
want to have a haven. We talk about criticism in our house and my wife had a story one time
where she said we shouldn't critique our children too harshly because the world will be
harsh enough on them. That was a profound phrase ... The world is hard enough when you
leave the protection of your home. You don't want people to be super unkind in the home
because at the end of the day the world can be unkind. I thought that was an amazing
epiphany."
