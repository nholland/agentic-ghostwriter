# Marriage as a Discipline of Perception

Many marriages don't collapse from events — they collapse from accumulated
interpretations. "He doesn't care." "She doesn't respect me." Once these
harden into identity-level judgments, they become prisons.

The Stoic question: *What am I making this mean?*
