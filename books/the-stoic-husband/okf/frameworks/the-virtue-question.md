# The Virtue Question (The Inward Turn)

The question: **"Am I being the best husband I can be?"**

It is the practical, first-person version of the Arête question. Arête is the
philosophical concept; the virtue question is the daily tool — what you ask in
the three-second window, in real time.

# Schema

The four virtues that define "best" map directly to the Stoic cardinal virtues.
The book does not need the Greek terms; the practical language does the work.

| Practical name | Definition | Stoic virtue |
|---|---|---|
| Logical | Thinking clearly before responding; not reacting from a first impression | wisdom / *phronesis* |
| Kind | Acting from genuine care rather than ego or score | justice / *dikaiosyne* |
| Self-controlled | Governing the impulse before it fires | temperance / *sophrosyne* |
| Brave enough to stay engaged | Not withdrawing or stonewalling; remaining present | courage / *andreia* |

# Placement

- **Introduction** — identity standard: "who is the man this marriage deserves,
  and am I becoming him?"
- **Chapter 1** — operational tool: the question asked in the three-second window.
- **Throughout** — returns as the book's reflective anchor for every scenario.
