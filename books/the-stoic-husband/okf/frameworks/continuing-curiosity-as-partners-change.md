# Author frame

"Your wife is always evolving, as are you. The key is to evolve together."

# Application

The author connects this to the river image and to losing interest in each other's changing shows, podcasts, and daily experiences. Shared development remains the author's aim. Whether this primarily means mutual curiosity, more shared experiences, or both is still an open interview question.

Separate preferences alone do not establish disconnection. Perel's discussion of otherness offers a useful qualification: a separate interest can supply something new to discover about a familiar person. This is research context, not a new ruling attributed to the author.
