# The 4Ds (Defensive Response Taxonomy)

The four automatic responses when a man feels attacked or criticized in a
marriage. All four are reactions to negative input that protect the ego at the
expense of the message. None of them engage with what's actually driving the
other person.

# Schema

| D | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Defense** | Attack back; catalog what the other person hasn't done. The intent is to hold up a mirror, but it escalates into both people defensively attacking each other. |
| **Deny** | Reject the other person's feedback outright ("No I have not"). In a world where things aren't empirical, this invalidates their experience rather than engaging with it. |
| **Downplay** | Criticize the intensity or delivery rather than engaging with the underlying message ("It's not that bad"). Creates a fractal of arguments because the conversation is now about degree, not the original concern. |
| **Deflect** | Redirect responsibility to an external source or another person; removes self-accountability entirely ("Why are you focused on me? Maybe you should focus on how much [person] is coming over here"). |
