# Romance as Disciplined Attention

Romance is not grand gestures or feelings. It is disciplined attention —
noticing, remembering what delights her, not taking her for granted, still
choosing.

Key line: *"Because I love by choice, I should make that choice visible through
action."*

# Related

- The "choice made visible" idea is the practical expression of
  [Virtuous vs. Performative Romance](/frameworks/virtuous-vs-performative-romance.md) —
  the same external behavior, sourced from character rather than anxiety.
- Connects to [Pursue Fully. Release the Outcome.](/frameworks/pursue-fully-release-the-outcome.md):
  disciplined attention is what "pursuing fully" looks like day to day.
- One of the five practices in
  [Daily Stoic Marriage Practices](/frameworks/daily-stoic-marriage-practices.md)
  (the midday act of care) operationalizes this.
