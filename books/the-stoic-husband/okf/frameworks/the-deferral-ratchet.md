# The Deferral Ratchet

Nobody decides to go silent for four years. He decides three times, each time
for a reason that was actually good.

**1. You're not in the mood to spend the energy.** The situation is tense and
you do not have a fight in you today. Reasonable.

**2. The moment is too good to spoil.** Everybody is laughing, and there is one
biting remark inside the laughing. Raising it would land like a brick on a
nice evening. It would be heavy, and over the top, and everyone would look at
you. Reasonable.

**3. She's too invested right now.** She cares about this more than you
expected, and bringing your piece into it would pull a lot of emotion into a
night that does not need it. Reasonable.

**Then the fourth arrives, and it is not a reason. It is a consequence.**

*It's too late to bring it up now.* You already let it go. If you raise it
today you are not raising one thing, you are explaining a delay — why it did
not bother you enough to say so then, and why it suddenly does now. You would
have to defend the timing before you ever got to the substance.

That is the ratchet. Each pass makes the next pass more expensive, because
each pass adds the delay to the thing you would have to justify.

# What Grows In That Room

Once the door is shut, the internal dialogue starts. *Let it go. Ignore it.
It's not that big a deal.* You turn around twice and there are sub-dialogues
running constantly, whole conversations conducted with a version of her that
is not in the room.

Then two things are true at once, and they are the reason this is a marriage
problem and not a private one:

- **It now feels genuinely frightening to raise anything**, because anything
  you raise arrives carrying everything you didn't.
- **She is lost.** This has been going on so long that she has no idea why
  tonight is the night. From where she sits, something small happened and
  the response did not match it.

# The Author's Own Position

He names this as his own pattern, not an observed one: an aversion to pain and
an aversion to fighting, so he does not bring things up early and often when
they are still small bites. Recorded because `01-voice.md` asks the author to
stage his own mistakes in real time rather than in retrospect.
