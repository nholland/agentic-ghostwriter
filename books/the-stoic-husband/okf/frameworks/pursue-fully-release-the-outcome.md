# Pursue Fully. Release the Outcome.

The clearest Stoic sentence about romance. Not half-hearted love — brave love.
Allows a man to:

- be romantic without becoming needy
- risk rejection without humiliation
- give without keeping score
- cherish someone without making them responsible for his soul
