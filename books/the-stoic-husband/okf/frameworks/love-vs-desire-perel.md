# Love vs. Desire (Perel)

"Love enjoys knowing everything about you. Desire needs mystery." Love moves
toward familiarity. Desire needs distance and otherness. The Stoic who pursues
fully but releases the outcome, who chooses without possessing, naturally
creates the conditions desire requires.
