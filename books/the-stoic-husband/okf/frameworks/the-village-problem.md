# The Village Problem

Modern partners are asked to provide what an entire village once provided —
meaning, soothing, identity, practical support, social life, erotic life,
friendship, and emotional regulation — all concentrated in one bond. When the
village collapses into one person, the stakes for every dimension become
catastrophic, especially erotic life.

This is the structure that turns a difficult situation into a crisis: a man in a
sexless marriage isn't only dealing with unmet desire — he's dealing with unmet
desire *plus* the loss of his only available source.

# Schema

- **Stoic side** (Seneca): the wise person desires friends but does not need them
  to complete him.
- **Perel side**: emotional monopolies destroy both freedom and love.
- **Convergence**: the more self-possessed each partner remains, the better the
  marriage functions.
