# There Are Two of You

In the author's words: "She said that she's studied relationships her whole life and that she's found one issue that's universal across all relationships. The biggest problem with all relationships is that there's another person on the other side of you, basically your counterpart. The whole play on words is that you have to realize that there are two of you. That's always going to be frustrating because you have to navigate somebody else's opinions, thoughts, and feelings."

"To me it brought a lot of humility to the situation because you realize that you won't always agree. You realize that you won't always think the same. You realize that relationships are all about negotiations, compromises, and finding ways to make a path together. It's not about being right. It's not about basically one person's demands or even one person's sacrifices."

He tied it to the dichotomy of control.
