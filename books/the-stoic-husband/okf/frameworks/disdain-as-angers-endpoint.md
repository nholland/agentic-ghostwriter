# Disdain as Anger's Endpoint

Unrepaired anger bouts don't disappear — they accumulate into a blur of feeling
rather than a traceable list of incidents. The blur solidifies into disdain:
"she doesn't respect me," "he doesn't value me" — felt but not located in
specific moments. Disdain is contempt's precursor, and contempt is what ends
marriages.

# Schema

- **Behavioral, not forensic:** Mental capacity isn't strong enough even in
  reflection to trace all the holes back to specific nails. The learning is
  therefore behavioral, not forensic. "You don't know which puff of the cigarette
  gave you cancer. The learning is that smoking leads to emphysema." The Stoic
  husband's job is not to reconstruct the history but to stop making holes.
- **The one-way street:** Without active recognition and repair, the
  accumulation moves in one direction — holes become blur, blur becomes disdain,
  disdain becomes contempt.
