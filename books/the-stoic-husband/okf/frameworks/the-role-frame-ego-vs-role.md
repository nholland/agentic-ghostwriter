# The Role Frame (Ego vs. Role)

# Schema

| Ego asks | Role asks |
|---|---|
| Am I being appreciated? | What does love require? |
| Am I winning? | What does justice require? |
| Did they admit I was right? | What is mine to repair? |

The move from ego to role is the move from consuming marriage to guarding it.
