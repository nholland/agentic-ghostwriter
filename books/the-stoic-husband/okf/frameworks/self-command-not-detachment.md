# Self-Command, Not Detachment

- "it put a label on the fact that being angry was a state of temporary madness. You were effectively out of control and a man who is powerful and strong is in control. A man who is effectively slavish to his emotions is weak"
- "It was also fascinating that I don't have to give in to the emotions of a situation. When someone is upset I am able to remain calm and balanced."
- "I think what Stoicism gives you are tools on how to navigate and to not be a slave to your emotions."
- "Stoicism gets a bad rap because a lot of times people can seem like they are unemotional or detached and I think that is a sign of immaturity in the study of the philosophy."
- **The correction (Round 3):** "I had a moment of reflection. I realized that I had to be doing something wrong because at the end of the day philosophy is about living a set of truths that should yield a good life." "I realized that my behavior and interactions actually made the situation worse and that's why I realized I needed to update how I was thinking about it"
- Story: [/stories/i-cant-control-you-said-aloud.md](/stories/i-cant-control-you-said-aloud.md).
- Source: [/citations/seneca-de-ira-1-1-temporary-madness.md](/citations/seneca-de-ira-1-1-temporary-madness.md) (Basore's English is "temporary madness"; Seneca reports "certain wise men" said it).

- **A mindset, not a line to say (Round 5, 2026-11-05):** "I think that I shouldn't have even brought her up when I said, 'I can't control you.' I think that that was part of the smug superiority." "'I can't control you' was a way of effectively saying how you're feeling and what you're doing and saying during our argument is something separate from me and something I'm uninterested in." "Whenever you think about the dichotomy of control and you're effectively saying, 'I can only control me,' it should be a mindset not something that you repeat out loud to another party. Effectively what you're trying to say is, 'I need to make sure that I hold my own actions to a standard that I'm happy with, that I control myself, that I hold myself to, or conduct myself in, a virtuous manner.' When you say something like, 'I can't control you,' it's a bit biting"
