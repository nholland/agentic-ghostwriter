# The Cigarette Close

You don't know which cigarette gave you the diagnosis. The learning isn't
forensic — it's behavioral. You don't need to trace every hole back to its nail.
You need to recognize the pattern and stop the behavior. "Smoking leads to
emphysema" is a more actionable learning than "that cigarette on March 14th
caused the tumor."
