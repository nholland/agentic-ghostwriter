# Attachment vs. Devotion

**Attachment** = needing another person to feel whole; peace depends on their
response. **Devotion** = chosen care that acts lovingly even when feelings
fluctuate, without using love as an excuse to become weak or dependent.

Key line: *"Stoicism rejects unhealthy attachment. It does not reject devotion."*

# Schema

| | Attachment | Devotion |
|---|---|---|
| Source | need | choice |
| Peace depends on | her response | own character |
| Under strain | collapses, controls | steadies, gives |

# Where a Stoic lives all day (author, Ch16 Round 1, 2026-11-04)

"Dispreferred and preferred are states where you exist almost all day long. It's the absolutes of
detachment or attachment that you effectively get to only a few times."

"I don't have to be unreasonably attached to her to the point where it drives anxiety or fear or
negative emotions. But I do prefer to have her happy. I do prefer to make her feel valued. I do
prefer to make her feel loved"

Round 2 wording of the slip: "of course, I don't want my wife unhappy."

# Keep it simple (author, Ch16 Round 5, 2026-11-05)

"just be careful to not make it too complex. I find it to be a pretty complex topic in general. The Stoics would say that nothing matters besides virtue and everything else becomes just a preferred indifference. I think that there is a hard nuance to walk here. What I'm trying to do (and to be clear I'm open to feedback) is to try to say that, in general, I'd like to conduct myself in a way that makes our marriage stronger, my wife happier, and helps us get towards eudaimonia"

Supersedes, for prose purposes, his Round 1 "states where you exist almost all day long" framing (which reviewers found read as a redefinition of Ch2's term).
