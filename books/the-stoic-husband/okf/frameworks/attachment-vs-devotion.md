# Attachment vs. Devotion

**Attachment** = needing another person to feel whole; peace depends on their
response. **Devotion** = chosen care that acts lovingly even when feelings
fluctuate, without using love as an excuse to become weak or dependent.

Key line: *"Stoicism rejects unhealthy attachment. It does not reject devotion."*
