# The Anger Spectrum

Anger exists on a spectrum of both triggers and manifestations.

# Schema

- **Triggers** range from catastrophic (betrayal, disrespect in front of
  children) to mundane (house is messy, partner watched the next episode without
  you, effort treated as scoreboard).
- **Manifestations** range from hot (verbal, volume, cursing — grandfather) to
  physical destruction (father) to cold (the cloud that hangs for days, silence
  that punishes without naming itself, sustained withdrawal that is anger in a
  different costume).

Key insight: The man who stonewalls or goes cold for three days may not
recognize himself in a chapter that only describes explosive rage. Cold anger
may be more corrosive over time because it's never named or addressed.

Both partners get angry when disrespected in front of their children — this is
the resource-defense trigger at full activation.
