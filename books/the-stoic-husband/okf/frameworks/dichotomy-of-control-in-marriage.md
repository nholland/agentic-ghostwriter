# The Dichotomy of Control in Marriage

Epictetus's dichotomy of control, translated into the daily texture of marriage.

# Schema

| Not yours | Yours |
|---|---|
| Spouse's mood | Patience |
| Spouse's tone | Honesty |
| Spouse's timeline for change | Restraint |
| Spouse's appreciation of your effort | Interpretation |
| Whether they apologize first | Willingness to listen |
| | Ability to repair |

# Core Reframe

*What kind of spouse am I becoming in response?*

This question moves the entire frame off the spouse's behavior — which cannot be
controlled — and onto the husband's own character, which can.
