# Preferred Externals (Terminology Handling)

The Stoic term "preferred indifferent" is technically accurate but risks being
heard as "indifferent to things that matter" — cold, uncaring.

Author's preferred framing: **preferred external** — things you prefer to exist
or be present (a good mood in the house, financial security, a warm welcome
home) but that are external to you and therefore outside your governing faculty.
Because they are external, their presence or absence does not have to determine
your inner state.

# Schema

| Standard Stoic term | Author's preferred term | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred indifferent | Preferred external | "Indifferent" risks reading as cold/uncaring — as if the Stoic husband doesn't care about things that matter |

Examples of preferred externals: a good mood in the house, financial security, a
warm welcome home.

# Placement

- **Ch 2 (stop outsourcing peace)** — terminology warning for the writer.

**Writer instruction:** Introduce the concept carefully; explain it before
naming it; use "preferred external" over "preferred indifferent" in prose when
the term is first introduced, to avoid the reader concluding that the Stoic
husband is simply indifferent.
