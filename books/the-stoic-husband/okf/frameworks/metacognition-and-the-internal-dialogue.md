# Metacognition and the Internal Dialogue

The "three-second gap" is a deliberately approachable name for a deeper
mechanism: metacognition — the practice of thinking about your own thinking.

Most people are on autopilot. When a stimulus arrives (a look, a tone, a
silence), they react without any awareness that a gap between stimulus and
response even exists. The internal dialogue is always there — it just runs
unheard. The three-second practice is training yourself to notice the internal
dialogue, not create one. You already have an inner voice. The practice is
learning to hear it before it gets drowned out by the reaction.

> "The 3-second gap is just a cheeky way of talking about it. What it really
> means is: you've got to listen to that inner dialogue. But you've got to train
> to hear it."
