# The Descent to Beast (Stoic Impulse Governance)

When a man gives in to impulse without governing it, he descends. The
distinction between a human being and an animal is the faculty of reason and
moral choice (*prohairesis*). A man enslaved to his reactions — his emotions,
his moods, the weather of the house — has surrendered that faculty and is
operating at the level of a creature that acts on instinct alone.

This is the Stoic framing for what self-governance is protecting against: not
just bad behavior, but the loss of what makes a man fully human.

# Schema

| State | Faculty | Level of operation |
|---|---|---|
| Governed man | Reason and moral choice (*prohairesis*) intact | Fully human |
| Man enslaved to reactions | *Prohairesis* surrendered to emotions, moods, the weather of the house | Like a creature acting on instinct alone |
