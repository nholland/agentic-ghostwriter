# Many Partners in One

In the author's words: "A buddy of mine told me one time that it's insane that we have monogamy because we're looking for a thought partner, a financial partner, a sexual partner, an entertainment partner, a friend companion, an activities partner, and we put all of this into one monogamous relationship. It's quite challenging. The unreasonable side is that we don't have a society where you go find a clear distinct person for each of those roles and so everybody has to live in a state of compromise. You might be highly compatible in multiple areas but highly incompatible in a few and you have to navigate those scenarios."

Also: "She's my best friend and I really think about all of the different partners that a lot of relationships require." The friend is never named.
