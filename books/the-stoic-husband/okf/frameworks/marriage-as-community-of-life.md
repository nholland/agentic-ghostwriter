# Marriage as Community of Life (Musonius Rufus)

Musonius describes marriage as a "community of life" — husband and wife
regarding all things as shared, the best marriages marked by "perfect
companionship and mutual love," each striving to outdo the other in devotion.

# Related

- The aspirational counterpart to [Bring Steadiness, Not Superiority](/frameworks/bring-steadiness-not-superiority.md) —
  community of life is the goal; steadiness without superiority is the posture
  that gets a Stoic husband there without turning the marriage into a contest.
- Echoed by [Marriage at Year 27, Clashing in the Kitchen](/stories/marriage-at-year-27-clashing-in-the-kitchen.md) —
  shared labor, even clashing labor, is community of life in practice.
