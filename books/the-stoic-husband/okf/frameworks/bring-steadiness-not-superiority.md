# Bring Steadiness, Not Superiority

The governing principle for a Stoic in a mixed marriage. Don't try to "win" by
being less reactive, more philosophical, or more disciplined than your spouse —
that becomes vanity wearing moral clothing.

# Related

- A guardrail on [The Virtue Question (The Inward Turn)](/frameworks/the-virtue-question.md) —
  the inward turn is for self-examination, not for keeping a private scoreboard
  of who is more enlightened.
- Connects to [The Role Frame (Ego vs. Role)](/frameworks/the-role-frame-ego-vs-role.md):
  using steadiness to feel superior is ego wearing the role's clothes.
