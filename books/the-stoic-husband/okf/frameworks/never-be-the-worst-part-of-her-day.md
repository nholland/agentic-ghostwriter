# Never Be the Worst Part of Her Day

In the author's words (Round 3): "Another thing that we say in our household is,
'Never be the worst part of somebody's day.' This is a way to make sure we spot check
ourselves and that we're not unkind. The counter or the corollary to that is, 'You can
be the best part of somebody's day.'"

His observation, not a statistic (do not print a number): asked "Are you the best part
of your wife's day?", he thinks most men would say that, most of the time, they are not.
"That's really what I'm trying to capture in this warmth arc."

Round 4: "I want to be the best part of her day."
