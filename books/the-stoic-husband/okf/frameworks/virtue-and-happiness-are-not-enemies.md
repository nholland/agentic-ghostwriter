# Virtue and Happiness Are Not Enemies

The right thing in marriage often feels like a loss in the moment. Over time,
moral acts — apologizing first, listening when misunderstood, telling the
truth — build trust, self-respect, and meaning. The man who becomes more
honest, loyal, compassionate, and dependable is building the conditions that
make durable marital happiness possible.
