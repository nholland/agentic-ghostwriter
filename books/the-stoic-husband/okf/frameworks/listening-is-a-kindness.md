# Listening Is a Kindness

The author (Round 4): "I think a lot of men don't want to hear the details of their
wife's day because maybe it's not interesting or it's mundane. I think that it's a
kindness to let them speak and get things off their chest." And: "a lot of men don't want
to listen to their wives talk. I think that just listening and being present is really a
kindness."

This is his observation about men in general, in his words, with no figure to print.
