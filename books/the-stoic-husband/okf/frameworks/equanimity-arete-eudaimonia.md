# Equanimity, Arete, Eudaimonia

In the author's words (Round 1): "when you are in equanimity as an individual, you are at peace. I think that's the key to the sun arc: you want your relationship to be in equanimity. There's another word, I think it's arete, that effectively means you're at your highest self. You're basically maximizing all of your potential and I think that phrase as well applies to your relationship. ... we want the relationship to be at its highest potential, to be at peace, and to be in a state of flourishing."

Round 2: "It was equanimity, arete, and then eudaimonia. Those are incredibly important to put in this chapter."

**Us over me (Round 1):** "Being happy as an individual is extremely different than being happy in a relationship. One requires thinking about both people and realizing that it's a joint decision and that the health of the whole is more important than the health of the individual."

**What he wants (Round 1):** "I want my wife to be loved. I want her to feel desired. I want her to feel like a priority."

**Prior use:** equanimity is defined in Ch1 and Ch2 (callback only). Arete and eudaimonia appear in no refined chapter as of 2026-11-04.
