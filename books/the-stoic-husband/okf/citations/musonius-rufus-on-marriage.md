# Musonius Rufus on Marriage

Musonius describes marriage as a "community of life" — husband and wife
regarding all things as shared; the best marriages marked by "perfect
companionship and mutual love," each striving to outdo the other in devotion.
He insists virtue and reason belong to women no less than men.
