# Marcus Aurelius — Meditations 11.18: They Act Involuntarily and in Ignorance

> "if they do what is wrong, clearly they act involuntarily and in ignorance — for as every soul is unwilling to be deprived of the truth, so is it unwilling not to be related to every man according to his worth."
> — *Meditations* 11.18, Farquharson trans.

# Why This Is Used

Ch3's turn: the person who wronged you did not set out to. Marcus is not excusing
the act, he is removing the premise that defensiveness depends on -- that the
other person is an adversary who meant it.

**Surrounding context:** Third item in Marcus's sequence of reminders for dealing with other people.
