# Why It Is Interesting For Ch12 And Still Probably Belongs Elsewhere

The chapter's best concrete instance is already a capitalization instance
without naming it: *"To her, love is you asking about the meeting at work
she's been dreading. Then asking again on Thursday."* Gable's finding is the
mirror image — what he does when the meeting went WELL.

The book has ten chapters left after this one, several of them about warmth
(Ch16) and joy (Ch27). This is a strong candidate for one of those and a weak
candidate for a chapter that is already at 1,202 words with a mechanism, an
anchor story, a sharp idea, a Stoic turn and an admission in it.
