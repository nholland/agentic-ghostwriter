# Source finding

Perceiving that a partner has invested in the relationship (time, effort, things they would lose) predicted feeling grateful, and gratitude predicted greater commitment, including among less satisfied participants.

# Limits

Romantic partners; marital status of samples unknown. 'Caused' is defensible only for the experimental study. Same lab family as Gordon et al. (2012), which Ch12 already uses; avoid a second appreciation-loop paragraph.
