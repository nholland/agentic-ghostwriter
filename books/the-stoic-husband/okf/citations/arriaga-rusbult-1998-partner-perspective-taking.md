# Source finding

As the abstract is reported in search: partner perspective taking, measured and in some studies manipulated, was associated with more positive emotion, more relationship-enhancing attributions and greater willingness to accommodate, independent of commitment and general perspective taking.

# Limits

About reactions to a partner's bad behavior, not gratitude for what one has. Fits Chapter 15 (patience and kindness) more naturally. Use in Ch14 only if 'perspective' is meant broadly, and say so.
