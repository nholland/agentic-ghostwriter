# DeMaris (2010) — The 20-Year Trajectory of Marital Quality

A six-wave panel study tracking 704 married respondents over 20 years,
testing whether equity theory's relevance to marital quality fades as a
marriage ages. It doesn't: felt equity remains associated with marital
quality across the full 20-year span, not just in early marriage. The
study also found women report subjective underbenefit (feeling they give
more than they get) more often than men do, at any given point in a
marriage's duration.
