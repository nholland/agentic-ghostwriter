# Marcus Aurelius — Meditations 6.8: The Ruling Reason Shapes Itself

> "The ruling Reason it is that can arouse and deflect itself, make itself whatever it will, and invest everything that befalls with such a semblance as it wills."
> — *Meditations* 6.8, Haines trans.

# Why This Is Used

Ch2's naming of the hegemonikon, the ruling faculty. This is the passage that
lets the chapter claim inner governance is a faculty a man already has rather
than a technique he must acquire.
