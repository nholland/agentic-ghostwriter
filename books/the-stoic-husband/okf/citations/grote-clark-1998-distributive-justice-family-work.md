# Grote & Clark (1998) — Distributive Justice Norms and Family Work

Distinguishes different norms people use to judge whether a division of
household labor is fair: equity (proportional to contribution), equality
(literally 50/50), need (whoever needs it more gets more help), and
communal (give according to what's needed, without keeping track). Both
spouses in the study rated the communal norm as the most "ideal" standard
for family work — even in couples whose actual day-to-day division didn't
fully live up to that ideal.
