# Hanson — "Taking In the Good" / HEAL Method (Hardwiring Happiness)

The brain encodes threats and negative experiences fast, by design —
survival favored the organism that overlearned danger. Positive
experiences don't get the same treatment; they have to be held in
attention for a stretch before they leave a lasting trace. Hanson's HEAL
method operationalizes that: have the positive experience, enrich it
(stay with it, notice its detail), absorb it (let it sink in), and
optionally link it against negative material. The mechanism is real and
well-supported; the specific "12 seconds" figure attached to it in
popular retellings is not confirmed here.
