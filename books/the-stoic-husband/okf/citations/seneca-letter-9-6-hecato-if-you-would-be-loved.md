# Seneca, Letter 9.6 (Gummere): Hecato's 'If you would be loved, love'

**What it says, plainly:** Hecato's recipe for being loved is to love first.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> Hecato says: 'I can show you a philtre, compounded without drugs, herbs, or any witch's incantation: "If you would be loved, love."'

# Cautions

- ATTRIBUTION DEFECT: the line belongs to Hecato, quoted by Seneca. Quote sites give it to Seneca. Never credit it to Seneca (research-kindness D).
- It is framed as a way to win love, which sits slightly against 'kindness irrespective of her.'
