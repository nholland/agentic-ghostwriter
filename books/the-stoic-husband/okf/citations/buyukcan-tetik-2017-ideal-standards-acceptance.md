# Finding

For average trait ratings, acceptance and satisfaction rose as the perceived
partner approached the person's ideals; once ratings met or exceeded those
ideals, additional match did not consistently add acceptance or satisfaction.
The result varied by method and partner effects were inconsistent.

# Limit

This is no numerical rule for deciding whether a particular spouse is “enough.”
It measures reported ideals and perceptions among newlyweds, not the ethics of
whether to raise a concern about aging, health, or money.
