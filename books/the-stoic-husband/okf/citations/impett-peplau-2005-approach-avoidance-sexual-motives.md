# Impett & Peplau (2005) — Approach vs. Avoidance Sexual Motives

Distinguishes two motivations behind sexual compliance (engaging without
one's own desire being the driver):

- **Approach motives** — having sex to obtain something positive: pleasure,
  intimacy, connection. Associated with more positive emotion, relational
  satisfaction, and general well-being.
- **Avoidance motives** — having sex to prevent something negative:
  a partner's disappointment, an argument, conflict. Associated with more
  negative affect, less satisfaction, less closeness, and more relationship
  conflict.

The sharpest finding: partners who frequently comply for avoidance reasons
report the **lowest relationship satisfaction of any group studied** —
worse than couples having sex infrequently. This is the direct empirical
case against treating intimacy as a duty to be discharged regardless of
willingness; obligation-motivated compliance is a risk factor, not a fix.
