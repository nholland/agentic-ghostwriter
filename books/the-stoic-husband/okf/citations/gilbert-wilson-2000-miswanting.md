# Gilbert & Wilson (2000) — Miswanting

The brain doesn't just chase the wrong things sometimes — it fails in
three specific, identified ways every time it forecasts future happiness:
picturing the wrong future event, using a flawed theory of what will
satisfy it, and misreading its own feelings once the moment arrives. The
predictable net effect is exaggerating both how good something will feel
and how long that feeling will last.
