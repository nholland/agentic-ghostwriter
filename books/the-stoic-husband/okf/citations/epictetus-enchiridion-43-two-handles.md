# Epictetus, Enchiridion 43 (Long): everything has two handles

**What it says, plainly:** Pick up the slight by the handle that says 'this is the person I love.'

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> Everything has two handles, the one by which it may be borne, the other by which it may not. If your brother acts unjustly, do not lay hold of the act by that handle wherein he acts unjustly … but lay hold of the other, that he is your brother…
