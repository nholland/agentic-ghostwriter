# Source finding

In a married sample, a spouse's felt gratitude predicted both partners' marital satisfaction; expressed gratitude predicted one's own satisfaction but not the spouse's.

# Limits

Correlational and small. Rare married sample, useful for Ch14's existing passage that quick thanks can land as hollow, but do not say 'saying thank you does not matter.' Confirm before use.
