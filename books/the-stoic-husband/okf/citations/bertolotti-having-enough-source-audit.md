# Source finding and limits

J.W. Bertolotti, A Stoic Guide to Happiness: The Art of Having Enough, Dying Every Day Day 129, November 11 2025. Screenshot and matching full article inspected. This is the exact intake source, distinct from the earlier /p/the-art-of-having-enough article.

Corrections: the full page says having, not loving; the initial screenshot reading is withdrawn. Seneca’s enough line and Epicurus’s Pythocles line are abbreviated paraphrases of Gummere. The nature/opinion thought reaches Seneca through Epicurus (Letter 16.7). Epictetus’s attributed sentence lacks a primary locator. REBT preferences/demands is supportable as theory, but the article’s synthesis is not a direct Ellis quote.

Exclude the blanket claim that extra wealth cannot increase happiness after basic needs: it provides no study and the book’s existing the-hedonic-treadmill concept already flags flat plateau claims as outdated. Do not import claims that each gratitude act increases freedom or that all three ancient schools share an identical aim as empirical or doctrinal conclusions. This article is a useful lead, not independent research evidence.
