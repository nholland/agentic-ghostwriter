# Source

Rituals and Nuptials: The Emotional and Relational Consequences of Relationship Rituals. Journal of the Association for Consumer Research 4(2). DOI 10.1086/702761. Full author-hosted paper read.

# Finding

Reported rituals were associated with satisfaction; the dyadic study found the association depended on agreement about having a ritual. This complicates the empty "Love you" example: shared meaning, rather than sheer frequency, is the useful question.

# Limits

The real-relationship studies are observational. Study 4 experimentally changes descriptions of a hypothetical couple, not the behavior of actual couples. The authors explicitly call for interventions to test causality. Do not prescribe a ritual as a proven remedy or claim routine affection is inherently empty. This is a candidate source, not an approved new author belief.
