# Source finding

The best-known Stoic 'negative visualization' about a spouse: remember what kind of thing each loved object is, from the earthenware jug upward; if you kiss your wife or child, say it is a human being, so their death will not disturb you.

# Limits

Recommend NOT using in Chapter 14. Out of context it reads as the emotional armor the book's premise warns against, and it makes the wife the object of a detachment exercise. If ever used (Ch27), it needs the surrounding Stoic account of love and duty.
