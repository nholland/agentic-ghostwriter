# Weiss (1973) — Emotional vs. Social Loneliness

Weiss's typology splits loneliness into two distinct kinds:

- **Emotional loneliness** — the absence of a truly intimate tie (spouse,
  lover, parent, child). Not fixed by company; fixed only by an intimate
  bond.
- **Social loneliness** — the absence of a network of peers/friends. Fixed
  by joining a community, not by one intimate relationship.

Weiss observed married people — including some with loving, attentive
spouses — who were still emotionally lonely. His conclusion, in
close-paraphrase: emotional isolation cannot be dissolved by entering other
kinds of relationships (more friends, more community); it requires the
intimate bond itself to change.
