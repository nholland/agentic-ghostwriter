# Source finding

As the abstract is reported in search: Investigation 1 found daters rated opposite-sex others less attractive than non-daters; Investigation 2 found this was not due to differences in own attractiveness, self-esteem, empathy, self-monitoring or altruism; the effect was specific to young opposite-sex targets.
