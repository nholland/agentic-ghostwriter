# Berridge & Robinson — Wanting vs. Liking (Incentive Salience)

The anticipation of a reward and the pleasure of receiving it run on
different circuitry. Dopamine drives the pull toward a goal — the
"wanting" — but the actual hedonic payoff once it's obtained is governed
by a separate, smaller, more fragile system. This gives a physiological
account for why the intensity of a chase (a promotion, a purchase, a
milestone) so often outpaces how good getting it actually feels: the two
were never running on the same system in the first place.
