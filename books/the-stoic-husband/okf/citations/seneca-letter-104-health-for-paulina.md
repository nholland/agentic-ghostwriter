# Finding

Seneca's wife Paulina urges him to guard his health. Though old age has made
him less afraid of dying, he attends to his own health more carefully because
his continued life matters to her. He calls such care for oneself, for a loved
one's sake, a kindness.

# Chapter limit

The passage concerns one's own care and reciprocal affection. It does not
prescribe a spouse's weight or authorize shaming a partner about appearance.
