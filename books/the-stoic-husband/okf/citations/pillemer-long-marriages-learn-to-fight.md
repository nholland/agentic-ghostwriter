# Pillemer — Long Marriages Don't Avoid Fighting, They Learn to Fight

The Cornell Marriage Advice Project — described as the largest in-depth interview
study of people in very long marriages — finds that elders explicitly teach *how*
to fight, not how to avoid conflict. Conflict management is a named category of
lessons; "not going to bed angry" appears as specific advice.

**Usable framing:** "The couples who stay married for fifty years didn't avoid
fighting." This is the book's claim — and 700 long-married elders are its
confirmation.

Study scale: 700+ individuals in marriages of 30, 40, 50, or more years
(combined ~40,000 years of marriage experience); a random national survey of
roughly 400 Americans aged 65+ plus in-person follow-up interviews with more
than 300 long-married individuals; average marriage length 44 years.
