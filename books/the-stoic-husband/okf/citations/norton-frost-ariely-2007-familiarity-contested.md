# Source finding

Norton et al. reported that more information about a person generally produced less liking, though people expected the opposite, attributing it to cascading perceived dissimilarity. Reis et al. found, with unacquainted strangers interacting live, that more interaction produced more attraction. A later integrative piece (Finkel et al., 2015, Perspectives on Psychological Science) treated it as an adversarial collaboration; not checked.

# Limits

Contested. Tempting for 'you desire what you do not understand' (the dream woman is ambiguous), but cannot be printed as 'familiarity breeds contempt' or its reverse. Strangers, not spouses.
