# Herbenick et al. (2014) — Desire Discrepancy as a Feature, Not a Bug

The paper's framing is itself the useful thing: desire mismatch between
partners is a **normal feature** of long-term relationships, not a bug to
be fixed or a sign the relationship or the partner is broken. The study
catalogs women's self-reported strategies for modulating their own desire
in long-term relationships — the existence of active, ordinary strategies
for managing the mismatch is itself evidence that discrepancy is the norm,
not the exception.
