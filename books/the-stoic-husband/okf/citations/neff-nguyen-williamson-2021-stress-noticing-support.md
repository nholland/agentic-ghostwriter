# Finding

In diaries from 121 married couples, husbands' chronic stress was associated with less accurate detection of wives' support needs; daily stress was associated with less support even when a need was perceived.

# Use and limits

Separate failure to notice from failure to respond. Overload is a possible complication to the chapter's complacency account. Direct gender-difference tests were nonsignificant: do not claim this is a uniquely male mechanism. Observational early-marriage sample; no causal claim. Support needs differ from sharing good news, so this supplements rather than replaces capitalization research.
