# Finding

The study compared 58 younger and 20 older couples predicting preferences. Longer relationships were associated with lower accuracy and greater overconfidence; overall accuracy still exceeded chance.

# Use and limits

A useful challenge to assuming familiarity guarantees knowledge. Age, cohort and relationship duration overlap, so this does not show that marriage makes people less knowledgeable, that spouses stopped asking questions, or that changing tastes caused errors. Use as a narrowly scoped candidate, not a causal headline.
