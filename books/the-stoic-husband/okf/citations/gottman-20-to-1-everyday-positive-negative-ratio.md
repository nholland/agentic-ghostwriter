# Source finding

The claim, as the search summaries state it: couples who stay together keep
roughly 20 positive interactions to every 1 negative in ordinary life, and
roughly 5 to 1 during a conflict conversation. Two different ratios for two
different settings; the 5-to-1 is the better-known one.
