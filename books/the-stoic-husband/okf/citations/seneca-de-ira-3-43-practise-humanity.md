# Seneca, De Ira III.43 (Stewart): the bull and bear, and 'let us practise humanity'

**What it says, plainly:** We are tied to the people we fight with. Be humane while you live among them.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> III.43.2 (fragment): "fastened together" … "we worry some one who is connected with us"

> Latin, as given in the research file: *aliquem nobiscum adligatum* (someone bound to us)

> III.43.5: while we live among human beings, let us practise humanity … Let us keep our tempers in spite of losses, wrongs, abuse or sarcasm
