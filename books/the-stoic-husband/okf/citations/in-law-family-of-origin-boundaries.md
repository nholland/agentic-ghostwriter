# In-Law and Family-of-Origin Boundaries in Marriage

The source listicle's framing: "Stop inviting your mom into your marriage.
This is a two-person build, not a group project." Pillemer's Cornell Marriage
Advice Project independently identifies in-laws as one of five major
stressors in long-term marriages, which corroborates that this is a real
theme. Ch9 ("Silence Is Not Peace") covers the adjacent but distinct
discipline of saying no to one's own spouse (the Nice Guy pattern), not
managing the marriage's boundary against extended family or corrosive
friendships — that's Chapter 10's job now.
