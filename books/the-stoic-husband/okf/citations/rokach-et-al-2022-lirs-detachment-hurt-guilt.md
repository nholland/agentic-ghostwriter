# Rokach, Sha'ked & Ben-Artzi (2022) — Loneliness in Intimate Relationships Scale (LIRS)

Developed and validated a self-report scale specifically for loneliness
experienced *within* an existing intimate relationship, distinct from
loneliness due to having no partner. Item generation drew on a
semi-structured questionnaire (108 participants), a second developmental
study (215 participants), and a validation study (306 participants), all
from the general Israeli population.

The final 14-item scale resolves into three factors:

1. **Detachment** (6 items) — feeling emotionally distant or disconnected
   from a partner despite physical proximity.
2. **Hurt** (4 items) — pain from unmet intimacy needs within the
   relationship.
3. **Guilt** (4 items) — self-blame or shame for feeling lonely despite
   having a partner.

Ami Rokach is also the author of an earlier, more narrative companion work,
*Together and Lonely: Loneliness in Intimate Relationships — Causes and
Coping* (with Ami Sha'ked; Nova, 2013), which may be a better source for
illustrative language than the 2022 validation paper itself.
