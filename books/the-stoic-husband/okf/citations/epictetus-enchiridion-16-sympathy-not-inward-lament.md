# Epictetus, Enchiridion 16 (Long): sympathy in words, not inward lament

**What it says, plainly:** Comfort the person, even grieve with him outwardly, but don't be carried away inside.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> So far as words then do not be unwilling to show him sympathy, and even if it happens so, to lament with him. But take care that you do not lament internally also.

**Evidence label:** PRIMARY READ at page-text, per section B7 of the research file. This
establishes wording only. Not `verified`.

# Possible use in Ch15 (proposal only; the author decides)

The compassion beat, as a complication rather than a model. The research file names Ch16 the stronger home.

- COMPLICATES: 'so far as words' can read as performed sympathy, the armor Ch16 exists to name.
