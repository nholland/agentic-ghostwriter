# Pillemer — Communication Is Paramount

Communication is the top lesson from the Cornell Marriage Advice Project. Elders
"overwhelmingly" recommend that couples "talk, talk, talk." Many whose marriages
had dissolved specifically blamed lack of communication as the root cause; the
elders believe most marital problems can be solved through open communication.

Carries external authority from 700+ individuals in marriages averaging 44 years
(combined ~40,000 years of marriage experience).

# Attribution warning

Do **NOT** attribute the phrase "don't sweat the small stuff" to Pillemer. That
phrase is Richard Carlson (*Don't Sweat the Small Stuff*, 1997) — a different
author, different context. Pillemer's research is about communication, commitment,
and friendship, not about minimizing small grievances.
