# Source

Reis et al. (2010), Are You Happy for Me? How Sharing Positive Events With Others Provides Personal and Interpersonal Benefits. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. Full faculty-hosted paper retrieved.

# Finding and limits

Experiment 3 compared sharing a positive event with an enthusiastic listener against a fun interaction and a neutral discussion. Relative to the fun condition, the capitalization condition produced greater immediate trust, perceived responsiveness and willingness to disclose more personal material. Do not claim every comparison with the neutral control was significant, or that all effects persisted unchanged at follow-up.

This provides a mechanism adjacent to the child's-picture example: how a listener responds matters beyond spending enjoyable time together. The listener was a laboratory confederate. This is not proof that husbands' inattention causes a years-long reciprocal withdrawal cycle in marriages.
