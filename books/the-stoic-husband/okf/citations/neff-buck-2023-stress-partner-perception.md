# Finding

The abstract reports a diary study of 79 newlywed couples: recent stressful life events were associated with closer tracking of negative behavior without equivalent sensitivity to positive behavior.

# Use and limits

Candidate qualification: paying attention to faults can coexist with little appreciative curiosity. That application is an editorial inference. Do not imply negative perceptions were imaginary, or that stress caused the observed difference. Abstract-level evidence only.
