# Source finding

2011: excluded participants in a ball-toss game judged social pain as more severe than included participants did; experience narrowed the empathy gap. 2015: people who had endured a distressing event (e.g., bullying) evaluated another's failure to endure it more harshly, partly because they remembered it as less difficult; they were more favorable to someone who succeeded; participants did not anticipate this.

# Limits

Not about marriage. For the writer's awareness: 'I did her job, so I appreciate her' can also become 'I did it, so why can't she.' If the chapter leans on firsthand experience, this is the honest qualification. Lead only for print.
