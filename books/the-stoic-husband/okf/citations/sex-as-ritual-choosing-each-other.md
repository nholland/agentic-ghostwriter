# Sex as Recurring Ritual of Choosing Each Other (Not Just Desire)

The source listicle's framing: "Have sex often. Not just for pleasure — but
as a reminder: you chose each other." This is a distinct angle from the
book's existing intimacy material, which runs through desire (Ch14, Perel's
love-vs-desire distinction) and absence (Ch17, the sexless marriage). The new
angle is regularity-as-affirmation — sex as a repeated, deliberate re-choosing
of the marriage, closer to a ritual than an appetite.
