# Pillemer — Friendship as Important as Love

The Cornell Marriage Advice Project elders explicitly name friendship — mutual
interests, free conversation, and the ability to "hang out together" — as
essential to long-term marriage. Physical attraction matters to sustaining a
relationship, but friendship runs alongside it, not below it.

This is the external validation for the friendship-as-infrastructure claim that
anchors Ch 24, carrying the authority of 700+ long-married elders (marriages
averaging 44 years).
