# Finding

In the authors' model, maintenance behavior can make a partner feel cared for,
gratitude can register that care, and gratitude travels with further
maintenance effort. This is relevant to the author's distinction between
contentment and stopping the work of love.

# Limit

The study does not define complacency, prescribe a frequency of romantic acts,
or show that one spouse can maintain a marriage alone.
