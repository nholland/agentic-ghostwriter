# Finding

The study separates accepting one's partner from feeling accepted by that
partner. Both relate to satisfaction in its dyadic model, but baseline
cross-sectional data do not establish that becoming more accepting causes
a marriage to improve.

# Limit

This source does not define contentment or complacency. The chapter's
distinction would be author synthesis, subject to approval.
