# Sisyphean Labor and the Loss of Meaning (Repetitive Undone Work)

During the Chapter 6 research check-in, the author described household
labor — laundry especially, with five people in the house — as work that
"never ends": it's finished, then immediately begins again. He recalled a
study where people were paid well (escalating pay) to do repetitive manual
work, and a researcher periodically destroyed the completed work and asked
them to redo it — and even high pay couldn't sustain motivation once the
work was revealed as pointless.
