# Evidence and attribution

Read the Gummere translation's online text, sections 22-23, and the collection title page identifying Richard M. Gummere. Seneca discusses human change and invokes Heraclitus's river. Heraclitus predates Stoicism; Seneca provides a direct Stoic reception of the idea.

The common modern sentence about the same river and the same man must not be presented as an exact ancient quotation from this source. Current intended use is paraphrase. Chapter application to spouses developing together belongs to the author, not Seneca's original argument.
