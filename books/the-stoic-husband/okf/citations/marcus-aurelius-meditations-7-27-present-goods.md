# Source finding

Long's Book VII text, passage conventionally numbered 27, tells the reader to attend to existing goods and consider how strongly they would be wanted if absent. It immediately warns against valuing them so excessively that their loss destroys composure. Both halves matter. This is philosophical counsel, not a psychological experiment or a statement about marriage specifically.

# Application limit

Supports attention and appreciation without possession or entitlement. The marriage application belongs to the author. Do not treat a wife as an owned object, or omit the warning against attachment.
