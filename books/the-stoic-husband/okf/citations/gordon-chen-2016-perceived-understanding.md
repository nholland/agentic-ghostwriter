# Gordon & Chen (2016): perceived understanding buffers conflict

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Across several studies including an experiment, frequent conflict hurt satisfaction only when people did not feel understood, and feeling understood in a conflict went with feeling better about the relationship afterward. Aggressive conflict and distressed couples were excluded.

**Evidence label:** SEARCH ONLY (section A4 of the research file). Nobody opened the paper.
