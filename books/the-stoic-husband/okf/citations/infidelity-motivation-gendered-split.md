# Infidelity Motivation — Gendered Split (Sexual Dissatisfaction vs. Emotional Neglect)

Secondary sources this session suggested a gendered split in self-reported
infidelity motivation: sexual dissatisfaction as the leading cause for men
(roughly half), emotional neglect as the leading cause for women (roughly
70%). A separate, better-sourced finding (Archives of Sexual Behavior
research on Ashley Madison users) complicates any simple causal story:
56% of men and 34% of women who had affairs rated their marriage "happy"
or "very happy" — undercutting the idea that infidelity is a clean,
predictable consequence of a sexless or low-frequency marriage.

# Status

**Unverified.** This is a real research question worth pursuing (it's

"drive" infidelity), but the specific percentages need a traceable primary
source before use. Treat as a candidate for a future, more careful
