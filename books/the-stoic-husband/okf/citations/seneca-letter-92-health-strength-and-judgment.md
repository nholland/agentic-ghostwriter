# Finding

Seneca says he will choose health and strength when he can, while the good
belongs to the judgment guiding that choice. He does not say that health is
worthless, or that another person's body may be treated as one's property.

# Chapter limit

Applying this distinction to a husband judging his wife's aging or weight is
the book's proposed application, not Seneca's stated example. No verbatim
wording is approved for manuscript use from this record.
