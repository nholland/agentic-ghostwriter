# Amor Fati

*Amor fati*, "love of fate": the disposition of not merely bearing what
happens but ceasing to be at war with it.

# The Provenance Problem

The phrase belongs to Nietzsche. It appears in *The Gay Science* (1882) and is
developed in *Ecce Homo*, and it entered modern Stoic-adjacent writing through
the popular revival rather than through the ancient texts. No Stoic wrote it.

The idea, on the other hand, is Stoic to its foundations, and there are two
clean primary passages for it. So the chapter loses nothing by teaching the
idea and leaving the label alone.

This distinction matters more here than it usually would, because the reader
this book is written for is exactly the reader who would catch it.
