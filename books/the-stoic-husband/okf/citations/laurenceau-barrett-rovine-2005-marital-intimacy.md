# Finding

In 96 couples' 42-day diaries, disclosure and perceived partner disclosure predicted same-day intimacy; perceived responsiveness partly mediated these relationships.

# Use and limits

Candidate for what happens after "How was your day?" Asking alone is not the whole process. Observational daily summaries cannot establish causal sequence; intimacy could also encourage disclosure. No promise that a wife will mirror renewed attention.
