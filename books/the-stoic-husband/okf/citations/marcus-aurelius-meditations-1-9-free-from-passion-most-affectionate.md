# Marcus Aurelius, Meditations I.9 (Long): Sextus, free from passion and most affectionate

**What it says, plainly:** Marcus remembers a teacher who never showed anger and was also the warmest of men; being with him felt better than being flattered.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> a benevolent disposition, and the example of a family governed in a fatherly manner … he had the power of readily accommodating himself to all, so that intercourse with him was more agreeable than any flattery … he never showed anger or any other passion, but was entirely free from passion, and also most affectionate

> Greek, as transliterated in the research file: *hama men apathestaton einai, hama de philostorgotaton*
