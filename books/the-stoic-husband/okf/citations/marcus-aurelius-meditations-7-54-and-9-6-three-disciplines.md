# Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VII.54 and IX.6 (Long): the three disciplines in his own words

**What it says, plainly:** Right now, judge well, treat the people near you justly, and accept what happens. That is enough.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> IX.6: Thy present opinion founded on understanding, and thy present conduct directed to social good, and thy present disposition of contentment with everything which happens--that is enough.

> VII.54: Everywhere and at all times it is in thy power piously to acquiesce in thy present condition, and to behave justly to those who are about thee, and to exert thy skill upon thy present thoughts…
