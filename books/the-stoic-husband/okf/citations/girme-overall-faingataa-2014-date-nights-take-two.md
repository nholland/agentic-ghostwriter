# Why This Is The Useful One

It is the finding that stops "date night" from being a box to tick, which is
precisely the objection the book's own audience research already records
(sources/audience-signals.md: date nights are "named as a fix but often feel
like a band-aid over disconnection"). A man who books a restaurant and spends
the evening elsewhere in his head has done the activity and not the thing.

That is the same claim the chapter already makes about the faucet: the act is
not the unit; what lands is.
