# Glover — No More Mr. Nice Guy (Nice Guy Syndrome)

Robert Glover's central claim: men who were taught (usually early, usually
unconsciously) that love and safety are conditional on being "good" become
compliant by default — avoiding conflict, hiding their real preferences,
and making covert contracts: doing something for someone with an unstated
expectation of a return, rather than asking directly for what they want.
When the unstated expectation isn't met, the result is resentment the Nice
Guy usually can't name, because he never stated the terms out loud in the
first place.
