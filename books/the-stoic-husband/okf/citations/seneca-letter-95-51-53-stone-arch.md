# Seneca, Letter 95.51-53 (Gummere): mutual affection and the stone arch

**What it says, plainly:** People hold each other up like the stones of an arch.

# Wording (as read; copied verbatim from the research file)

> It is indeed worthy of great praise, when man treats man with kindness!

> Nature produced us related to one another … She engendered in us mutual affection … let our hands be ready for all that needs to be helped.

> Our relations with one another are like a stone arch, which would collapse if the stones did not mutually support each other
