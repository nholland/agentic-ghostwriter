# Saxbe, Repetti & Graesch (2011): housework, leisure and recovery from work

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Activities were logged through the evening and saliva cortisol was sampled repeatedly. Wives' evening cortisol recovery was healthier when husbands did more housework. Husbands' leisure went with healthier recovery, but only when their wives spent less time in leisure (the press release's wording). Doing housework kept evening cortisol higher for both spouses.
