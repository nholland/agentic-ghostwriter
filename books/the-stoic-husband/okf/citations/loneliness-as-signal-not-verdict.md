# Loneliness Inside a Marriage as a Signal, Not a Verdict

The source listicle's framing: "You can feel lonely in a relationship. It
doesn't mean it's wrong — it means something needs attention." Related to,
but narrower than, existing material: Ch18 diagnoses the "roommate marriage"
pattern from the outside, and Ch3's central story shows the wife experiencing
loneliness after a specific interaction. Neither currently states the direct,
reader-facing reframe: *if you feel lonely in your own marriage, that is
data, not a verdict on the relationship or the choice you made.*
