# Brené Brown — Clear Is Kind, Unclear Is Unkind

Brown's formulation, from *Dare to Lead*: being vague with people in order to
spare them is not kindness. Her research on leadership found that most people
avoid clarity believing indirectness is gentler, when the effect is the
opposite. She has said she picked the saying up in a twelve-step meeting about
two decades before the book, which means the idea is not original to her even
though the phrasing is the version everyone now quotes.

# Why this is in Chapter 10

The chapter's counterintuitive claim is that stating a line out loud is a form
of kindness rather than a withdrawal of it, which is what keeps a chapter about
saying no from reading as armor. The author supplied that framing himself
during the Chapter 10 restructure ("clarity is kindness"), and it went into the
draft unattributed.
