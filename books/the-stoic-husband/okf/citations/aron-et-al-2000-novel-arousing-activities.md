# Why It Matters

The chapter's prescription is currently priority and form. This adds content:
what the time together is spent ON is not neutral. The same evening, spent on
something new, does something the same evening spent on the usual thing does
not. That is a better answer to "so what do I actually do" than "go on a
date," and it is the research answer to the author's rented planes.

# The Risk

01-voice.md allows one concrete image per chapter, and Ch12's is the muscle.
Self-expansion is itself an image (the self getting bigger) and must not be
imported as one. Use the finding, not the metaphor.
