# Gottman, Coan, Carrère & Swanson (1998): accepting influence and de-escalation (contested)

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Per the UW press release headline, husbands who accepted influence from their wives ended up in happy, stable marriages. Secondary summaries add that husbands' de-escalation of wives' low-intensity negativity predicted stability, that positive affect soothed the partner, and that active listening did not predict outcomes.
