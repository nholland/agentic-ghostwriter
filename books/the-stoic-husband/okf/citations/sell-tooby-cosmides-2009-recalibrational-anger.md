*PNAS*, 2009 — cited, not quoted.

# Why This Is Used

Ch4's evidence that anger is a bargaining mechanism -- it fires to recalibrate
how much weight someone gives your interests. That framing is what lets the
chapter argue anger signals lost leverage rather than strength, without moralizing.
