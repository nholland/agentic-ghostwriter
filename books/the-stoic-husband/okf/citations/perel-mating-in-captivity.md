# Perel — Mating in Captivity (2006)

Perel's core argument: modern couples want safety and transcendence, intimacy and
erotic risk, all concentrated in one bond; desire requires mystery and otherness.

Key concept: *"Love enjoys knowing everything about you. Desire needs mystery."*
