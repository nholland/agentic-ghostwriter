# Gottman — The Four Horsemen

Gottman research identifies four behaviors most predictive of divorce:
**criticism, contempt, defensiveness, stonewalling.**

The Stoic marriage framework addresses all four directly: contempt (Ch 4, 5),
defensiveness (Ch 3), stonewalling (Ch 5), criticism (Ch 7).

# Primary source, and a statistic the book must not use

The `resource` above is the Gottman Institute's own article, confirmed by the
2026-09-06 external check below. The underlying peer-reviewed work is:

Gottman, J. M., & Levenson, R. W. (1992). "Marital processes predictive of
later dissolution: Behavior, physiology, and health." *Journal of Personality
and Social Psychology*, 63(2), 221-233. doi:10.1037/0022-3514.63.2.221

**The "90%+ accuracy" figure does not survive cross-validation.** Popular
sources repeat it constantly (often as "93.6%"). It comes from equations
fitted to couples whose outcomes were already known, then evaluated on that
same data. Tested against fresh samples, positive predictive value falls to
roughly 21-29%:

Heyman, R. E., & Slep, A. M. S. (2001). "The hazards of predicting divorce
without crossvalidation." *Journal of Marriage and Family*, 63(2), 473-479.
doi:10.1111/j.1741-3737.2001.00473.x

This is the specific mechanism behind the standing bar recorded in the
External Verification block below, which reached the same conclusion
independently. Cite the four behaviours, name Gottman, make no numerical
claim.
