# Pillemer — Don't Keep Score

The Cornell Marriage Advice Project elders advise against treating marriage as a
50/50 proposition. The successful framing is that both partners try to *give
more than they get* — rather than tracking inputs and outputs or demanding
equity.

This carries external validation from 700+ long-married elders (marriages
averaging 44 years) for the book's argument against scorekeeping.

phrase is Richard Carlson (*Don't Sweat the Small Stuff*, 1997) — different
author, different context.
