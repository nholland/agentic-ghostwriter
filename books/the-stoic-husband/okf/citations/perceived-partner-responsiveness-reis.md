# Perceived Partner Responsiveness

Three beliefs, held by the person receiving: he understands me; he thinks what
matters to me is worth something; he acts on it. Note what is NOT in the
definition — the format of the act. Responsiveness is about accuracy of
attention, not about which of five channels the care travelled down.

# Why It Is The Right Frame For The Love-Languages Ruling

The author's ruling keeps the five-language taxonomy as a thinking tool while
declining to treat it as science. This construct is what makes that ruling
printable. It supplies the honest version of the load-bearing half — whether
care lands depends on what the receiver takes from it, not on what the giver
spent — without borrowing Chapman's authority for it.

It also means the chapter does not need to name the construct. 04-archetype.md
sets citation density low. The idea carries fine in plain English, and the
chapter already does that ("it arrived in a form she doesn't read as love").
