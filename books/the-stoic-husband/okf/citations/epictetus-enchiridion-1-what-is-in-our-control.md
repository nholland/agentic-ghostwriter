# Epictetus — Enchiridion 1: What Is and Isn't in Our Control

> "Some things are in our control and others not. Things in our control are opinion, pursuit, desire, aversion, and, in a word, whatever are our own actions. Things not in our control are body, property, reputation, command, and, in one word, whatever are not our own actions."
> — *Enchiridion*, Ch. 1, Carter trans.

# Why This Is Used

Ch2's foundational statement of the dichotomy of control -- the passage the whole
chapter argues from. Distinct from /citations/epictetus-enchiridion-33-disciplined-speech.md,
which is chapter 33 of the same work and covers speech, not jurisdiction.
