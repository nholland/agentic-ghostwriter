# Source finding

Gratitude interventions outperformed measurement-only controls on well-being with a small effect; against active alternative-activity controls the effect on well-being was small. A separate later meta-analysis (Cregg & Cheavens 2021) reported small effects on depression and anxiety symptoms (not checked here).

# Limits

Calibration, not prose. No printed claim about gratitude practice should exceed the chapter's existing 'a reason to practice on purpose.'
