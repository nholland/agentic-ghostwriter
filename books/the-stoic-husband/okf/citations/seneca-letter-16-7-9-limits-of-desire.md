# Source finding and limits

Gummere online text directly read. Section 7 explicitly credits Epicurus for the nature-versus-opinion maxim; 8–9 distinguish bounded natural wants from indefinitely expanding desires formed by false opinion. Section 9 asks whether the object of pursuit has a definite stopping point.

This is the most useful additional ancient diagnostic for the mindset of enough. Possible marriage application: recognize a comparison standard that keeps moving. That application is proposed, not ancient marriage advice, and is not a test for whether a legitimate concern should be voiced.
