# Finding

Matching one's intrinsic ideals, such as warmth and intimacy, was more strongly
associated with relationship functioning than matching extrinsic ideals such
as attractiveness and success. When intrinsic ideals were well met, extrinsic
match mattered less in this sample. This supports *asking which standard is
doing the work* rather than asserting appearance or money never matter.

# Limit

Do not write that this proves long-married husbands stop caring about looks
or income. The sample was young and largely female; the design was not an
experiment. No threshold of “enough” for an individual marriage was measured.
