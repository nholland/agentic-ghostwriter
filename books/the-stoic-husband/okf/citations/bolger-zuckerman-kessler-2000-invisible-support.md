# What It Buys The Chapter

An answer to a reader who thinks "I do plenty for her and she doesn't see it"
is a complaint about her. It is a measured phenomenon: in couples under
stress, a large share of what one partner recorded doing did not appear in the
other's record of the day at all. That is not ingratitude and it is not
imagination. Giving and registering are two events.

# What It Does Not Buy

It does not say people have channels. It does not support a taxonomy. It
supports the weaker, sturdier claim underneath one.
