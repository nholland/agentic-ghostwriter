# Finding

Perel and Mary Alice Miller describe curiosity toward a partner's separateness and seeing a familiar partner in fresh settings. The chapter implication is to distinguish separate interests from indifference to those interests.

# Limits

Clinical/editorial perspective. Does not prove that time apart increases desire in every couple. Does not prescribe identical interests or guarantee a response to renewed pursuit.
