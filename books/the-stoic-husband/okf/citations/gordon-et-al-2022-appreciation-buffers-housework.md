# Gordon et al. (2022): feeling appreciated buffers unequal household labor

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Several samples collected during COVID-19. Doing more housework and seeing the division as unfair went with lower satisfaction; the association disappeared among people who felt appreciated. Reported as holding across gender, employment, age and relationship length.
