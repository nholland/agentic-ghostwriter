# McNulty & Russell (2010): when negative behaviors help

**What it reports, as far as search could tell:** Minor problems: less blaming went better. Serious, frequent problems: direct engagement, even negative, went better. McNulty is quoted in coverage: if problems are rare, give the benefit of the doubt; if serious and frequent, don't look the other way (paraphrase of coverage).
