# The Vortex

In the author's words: "Disconnection often happens when everyone is busy and there's nothing but a mountain of work ahead of you. It gets even worse whenever other family members might have friction between them. If my wife is frustrated with one of her siblings on the phone or if she's frustrated with one of our children, that then leads to disconnection with us. Sometimes I have to see that as not a verdict on me but just a sign that she is feeling strained. Whenever I take the bait I add to the overall layer and then everybody's pulled into the vortex."
