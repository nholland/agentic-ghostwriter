# The Old Blueprint of a Man

In the author's words: "Men were fearless. They were protectors. They were providers and they provided for their families." And: "The irony is that for me, when I think about the men in my family, a lot of them were quite volatile. Men were loud and they yelled. They often were very physical and they broke things around the house if they were angry. It was a way to show that they were powerful. My sense is that most of the men I grew up around would have thought it way too weak to talk about your feelings. A man bore things quietly or if he finally was pushed to the edge it was okay for him to lose himself in anger and blow up."

"A lot of men that I know basically kept quiet until they made a demand. There wasn't a lot of nuance there. ... The reality is you basically made demands, you had benign neutral conversation, or you kept quiet. This was the same for my grandfather and my father. Their word was the law and if they were challenged in any way they both were extremely volatile, with curse words, yelling, screaming, and occasionally throwing things around the house."
