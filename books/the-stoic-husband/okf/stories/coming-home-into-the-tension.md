# Coming Home Into the Tension

In the author's words (Round 4), condensed only where marked:

When he gets home from work he sometimes finds himself "put on the defense because as
soon as I get in the door my wife is frustrated with the children or things are hectic."
There is "a string of interactions that are slightly tense," in which she is "asking for
the status of things that need to be done" and asking him to put food on the grill or
start folding clothes.

He would feel tired, and frustrated "that immediately she was giving me things to do." If
he wanted to relax, or was sitting down talking to the kids, she would say things like
"We don't have time to just lay around and not do stuff," or "There's an infinite amount of
work to get done. Just look around. I shouldn't have to tell you what to do."

Those lines "would originally put me on the defense and make me feel like she was saying I
was lazy." Over time he realized "it's more a verdict on how stressed and overworked she
is." Instead of giving in and retaliating: "I need to roll up my sleeves and start helping,"
and "give her a hug, tell her I see her, and spend a little bit of time seeing if I could do
something to make her day lighter."

# Widened by the author, 2026-10-23 (Round 5)

Author: "Maybe we don't do the 'coming home to the stressed partner' framing because this also
happens on Saturday mornings: she wakes up and she has a jam-packed agenda for Saturday and
Sunday to get a lot of things done. It's the same exact scenario, just not in the evening. It's
busy weekends."

So the pattern is not the time of day or the doorway. It is her full agenda meeting his rest,
with a remark that sounds like a verdict on him and is a reading of her load. The file name
keeps "coming-home" so existing links hold; the title and framing follow the author.

The research on returning home from work (Repetti, Schulz, Campos, Saxbe) fits the evening
version only. Dyadic coping and appreciation (Bodenmann; Gordon 2022) fit both. Nothing tests
the weekend version. Source: Author interview, Chapter 15 (runs/ch15/interview.md), Round 5, 2026-10-23 15:14.
