# Author testimony

The author loves that his wife wakes at small nighttime sounds and wakes him
to check them. She is a morning person who gets the household up. She saves
things he would discard, sometimes even retrieving them from the trash; they
bicker about small items. He loves her new gray hairs, which she does not want
others to see. She was a high-powered executive with a large income and has
taken a sabbatical to be a full-time mother. She has a “wicked green thumb.”

> She is enough for me, despite how our bodies change, despite how she was a high-powered executive and earned tons of money, and now she has taken a sabbatical and she is a full-time mom with a wicked green thumb. She is enough and it takes tons of work to make sure that you realize that the discipline of enough is critical.
