# Author words (verbatim excerpts; full text in runs/ch16/interview.md)

- **Reframe (Round 0, pending outline ruling):** "I think this is too on the nose for stoicism ... not be 100% tied to stoicism ... go more with the lack of emotions, or their traditional confusion around what it means to be a man."
- **Lost boys, 2026:** "you have a lot of lost boys who don't know how to navigate: What does it mean to be a man? If you are too much into your feelings, you are often seen as weak and whiny by women but if you're too aloof and you effectively are too volatile, you're seen as dangerous and unapproachable." "It's a thin line between being vulnerable and being detached." (Pushback asked Round 2: observed, or men's fear?)
- **What Stoicism gave him:** "it put a label on the fact that being angry was a state of temporary madness ... a man who is powerful and strong is in control. A man who is effectively slavish to his emotions is weak." Also being kind lets him "communicate more directly what I'm feeling, what I'm thinking, what I'm expecting, and I don't have to feel like it's being mean."
- **Superiority cloak:** "Being self-controlled in a situation is different than being detached or even smug. Sometimes people wear their philosophy and their religion like a moral superiority cloak."
- **Why he wants her happy:** cosmopolis and the virtue of justice; "so I want her happy." (Round 1 text reads "of course, I don't want my wife happy"; treated as a slip, confirmation asked Round 2.)
- **Preferred indifference as daily life:** "Dispreferred and preferred are states where you exist almost all day long. It's the absolutes of detachment or attachment that you effectively get to only a few times." "Stoicism gets a bad rap ... a sign of immaturity in the study of the philosophy."
- **Two people:** a woman on Facebook (unnamed, unattributable) said the universal problem in relationships is that there is another person; to him it "brought a lot of humility ... It's not about being right."
- **Many partners in one:** a friend (unnamed, disclosure boundary) on monogamy asking one person to be thought, financial, sexual, entertainment, friend and activities partner. Placement asked Round 2 (here or Ch26).
- **Us over me:** "Being happy as an individual is extremely different than being happy in a relationship ... the health of the whole is more important than the health of the individual."
- **Closing the Sun arc (proposal):** equanimity, arete, and a third word for flourishing he could not recall (eudaimonia proposed, unconfirmed). "We want the relationship to be at its highest potential, to be at peace, and to be in a state of flourishing." "I want my wife to be loved. I want her to feel desired. I want her to feel like a priority."
- **Joy:** "I feel deep joy and deep love for my wife ... I love walking the block with her. I love going to the movies with her. She's my best friend."
