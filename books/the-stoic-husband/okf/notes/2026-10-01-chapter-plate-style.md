# Presented proposal

The Publisher recommended:

- Black on white, with Georgia type.
- A consistent title, subtitle, label, and caption hierarchy.
- A standard width, with height fitted to the drawing.
- At most three line weights.
- Explicit text alignment and margins checked in the rendered image.

The Publisher specified that arc-specific rules stay parked, then asked:
“Approve this chapter-plate style?” The author replied:

> Yes

# Decision

The five chapter-style principles are approved and written into
books/the-stoic-husband/design/plates/README.md. The existing chapter convention
supplies a 640-unit width and the existing dark ink/type hierarchy. Height is
fitted to the content. The approval does not ratify the full older style
proposal or approve any particular diagram. Caption limits, fixed symbol
meanings, and the drawn Lesson requirement remain pending in inbox 066.
Arc/Part style questions remain parked under the arc-completion ruling.

# Copy, drawn Lesson and symbol meanings — inbox 066

The Publisher recommended limiting copy to a subtitle, one short gloss per
element and one closing line; having the drawing convey the lesson and helpful
response; and deriving symbol meanings from each chapter's metaphor and labels.
The exact ruling question was: “Approve the first two rules and decline a fixed
book-wide symbol dictionary?” The author replied:

> approved

The copy cap and drawn Lesson requirement are accepted. The proposed universal
symbol dictionary is declined. Record the distinction: the earlier Reader Panel
recommendation to adopt all three is not the author's ruling. These are chapter
rules, with Arc/Part work still deferred. Existing diagram review status is
unchanged; no bulk redraw is commissioned. The counted excess-caption row is
now FAIL for chapters; Arc checks retain their prior advisory status. The
meaning of the drawn Lesson remains a Reader Panel review responsibility.
