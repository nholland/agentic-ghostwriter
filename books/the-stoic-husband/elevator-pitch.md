# Elevator Pitch — The Stoic Husband

**Calm as a river. Strong as an oak. Warm as the sun.**
**River, Oak, Sun: The Stoic Husband.**

**River.** He doesn't stop flowing when something gets thrown into the water. A sharp comment. A bad night. A hard season. None of it dams him up or sends him crashing over the banks. He absorbs it and keeps moving. Calm isn't the absence of current. It's command of it.

**Oak.** He carries the weight without resentment and without bending into someone smaller. Storms. Drought. Twenty winters in a row. None of it stops him from growing. He's load-bearing because he's built to hold it.

**Sun.** He keeps giving off warmth whether the day is clear or covered in cloud. Her mood, the state of the house, how the week is going. None of it decides whether he shows up loving. The sun doesn't wait for good weather to shine. Neither does he.
