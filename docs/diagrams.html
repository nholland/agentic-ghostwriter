A book production house, in three pictures

What we learned building a writing system

These are the three decisions that mattered. Each one came from a failure that shipped first, and each is the part a second team would otherwise have to rediscover.

Drawn from a working system: a book production house where the author is in the room for two things - saying what the chapter is, and saying whether it landed - and everything between runs as cold sub-agents and scripts that cannot be talked past.

Each figure is a standalone SVG. The drawings are the source and this page is generated from them, which is the third diagram applied to itself.
