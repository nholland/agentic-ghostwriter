# tactics-review.md and the practice guide overlap - merge or retire?

Two reader-facing artifacts cover the same ground. appendix/practice-guide.md (generated by every refine, accumulates, plain numbered practices per chapter) and tactics-review.md (2,075 words, hand-made 2026-08-23, covers Ch1-8, referenced by nothing but LEARNINGS.md) - per chapter: the Stoic lesson, the pattern it corrects, and the practices marked Proactive or Reactive. The tactics review is the more polished read and explicitly 'meant to be read on its own'. The practice guide is the one a command maintains. Both are stale or partial against eleven refined chapters.

**What unblocks this:** Either (a) tactics-review is the reader-facing polish of the practice guide, in which case one generates the other and the Line Editor's section format should carry the Proactive/Reactive marking; or (b) it was a one-off and should be retired to an archive. Right now both exist, neither is current, and no desk owns the second.
