# "The Platinum Rule" is a registered trademark. Decide before it reaches a title or a Substack headline.

The concept is Milton Bennett's, coined 1979 in 'Overcoming the Golden Rule: Sympathy and Empathy'. But 'The Platinum Rule' as a phrase is a registered trademark of Tony Alessandra, attached to a commercial assessment product. Using the idea in prose with attribution to Bennett is fine and the chapter does that. The risk is downstream: a chapter title, a subtitle, a Substack headline or a marketing asset reaching for the phrase, in a book you are self-publishing, where the rights risk is yours and not a publisher's. Raised now rather than at marketing time because title decisions happen after the draft exists and the phrase will be sitting right there.

**Recommendation:** Keep it in prose with the Bennett attribution, and keep it out of the chapter title and every marketing asset. The idea is what you ruled in; the phrase is someone else's product name.
