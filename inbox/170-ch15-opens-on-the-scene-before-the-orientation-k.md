# Ch15 opens on the scene before the orientation: keep the exception, or restore 'Orient before the scene'?

Rule: 01-voice.md says 'Orient before the scene. Every chapter opens with 1-3 sentences of orientation before the first story or scene drops.' What changed (QA item Q058, batch 5, landed 2026-11-10 in commit 4c104a6): Ch15 used to open 'Warmth is easy on a good day. It gets tested on the ordinary ones, when nobody is at their best.' then listed the three tests and their answers ('Each one has an answer: meet hostility with kindness, frustration with patience, and disconnection with warmth.') before the zing scene. The Panel and Anti-Slop Reader called that a lesson plus a table of contents. Ch15 now opens: 'It usually starts small. She says something with an edge about the things that need doing, and before you've decided anything, your answer is already in your mouth.' The orientation follows it, then 'Those days test it three ways: she's sharp with you, you're both fed up, or a busy day pulls you apart.' The 'Each one has an answer' list is gone from the opening; the three answers remain the chapter's section labels. Case for keeping: 'It usually starts small' orients as well as it shows. Case for the rule: every other chapter orients first, and the spec has no exception clause. Author, 2026-11-10: 'Exception is ok for now, but note with detail in the Inbox.' No spec text was changed.
